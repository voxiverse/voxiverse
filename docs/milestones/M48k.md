# M48k: first-person hands, held blocks and voxel gun models

Owner-decided (issue #60), for the LAN party, first among the open rows (before M49). Built
exactly as the owner described and nothing more: no other items' models, no new effects or
sounds, no change to the weapons' behaviour. Where this spec had to decide something the
issue does not state, the decision is in "Decisions" below, with the principle it follows.

## What the player sees

**First person.** The avatar's own right arm (the same boxes as `avatar_draw`'s: a sleeve
`{-0.075,-0.4,-0.075}..{0.075,0.1,0.075}` in the shirt colour, a hand
`{-0.075,-0.65,-0.075}..{0.075,-0.4,0.075}` in `SKIN`, so the proportions, the shirt of
`shirt_of(name)` and the skin are the avatar's) comes up from the lower right of the screen
and carries what the hand holds. What is held decides how it is drawn (`Held_Kind`):

| Held | Drawn as |
|---|---|
| nothing | the arm alone, forward |
| an item that places a block (cubes, stairs, slabs, doors, fences, plants) | **the block's real mesh**, small (`HELD_BLOCK_SCALE` 0.24 of a block): `mesh.mesh_chunk` over a one-block snapshot, so its shape and its textures are the world's |
| an item with a `model` (the four weapons) | the **voxel model**, `HELD_VOXEL` (0.04 block) a voxel |
| any other item (tools, materials, armor) | an **extruded icon**: the item's 16 x 16 sprite (the art `icons.odin` draws it from), each opaque pixel a voxel `HELD_PIXEL` (0.025 block) wide and `HELD_THICKNESS` (2) pixels thick |

The view model is drawn **in its own pass** after the world, entities and sky: its own
projection with a fixed vertical field of view (`VIEW_MODEL_FOV` 60 degrees, the window's
aspect, nothing from the FOV setting, the sprint kick or the zoom), and the depth buffer
cleared to the far plane first, so it never clips into walls. It is lit by the world's
scene (the sky light of the time of day, the brightness setting), without fog.

**Third person** (other players, and you in F5): the same models, built by the same
functions, in the avatar's right hand: the hand's end is the grip, the model's front is the
direction the arm points. The arm is raised (`AVATAR_RAISED`) while holding anything and
level with the ground (`AVATAR_AIMED`, about 1.5 radians) while holding a weapon.

**Animations** (`Vm_State`, pure: `vm_event` and `vm_update` give a `Vm_Pose`; the session
feeds it; nothing animated is sent):

| | |
|---|---|
| swing | on the attack or break button with anything that is not a hitscan weapon: 0.28 s, an arc down and across |
| place | on a use press that places or uses: 0.22 s, a push forward and down |
| recoil | each shot of a weapon (the shot the client predicts, `session_shoot`) and each rocket: a kick back and up that decays (`VM_RECOIL_DECAY`), adding up under automatic fire to a cap |
| reload dip | from the reload's start for `reload_time` seconds (the weapon's data): the model drops, tilts and comes back |
| idle sway | a slow breath, a few thousandths of a block |
| walk bob | from the camera's own bob phase and amplitude (`s.bob_phase`, `s.bob_amp`) |
| sniper | **hidden while zoomed** (the weapon has `zoom` and `s.zoom` is above `VM_SCOPE_HIDE`), and a scope overlay is drawn on the UI layer: black outside a circle, thin cross lines, faded in with the zoom |

The idle sway and the walk bob are **off when "View bobbing" is off or Reduce motion is
on**. The swing, place, recoil and reload motions are not bobbing and play regardless: they
are the feedback of an action, as the recoil kick already is. **F1** hides the view model
with the HUD (`app.hud_hidden`); the scope overlay is HUD too.

## Voxel models are data

A new registry kind `Model`: `data/models/<name>.json`, id `<pack>:<name>`, several to a
file, like items:

```json
{ "pocket_gun": {
    "size": [4, 3, 1],
    "palette": ["#2b2d33", "#6f7480", "#a5acb8"],
    "slices": [["2211", "..11", "..1."]],
    "grip": [2, 1, 0] } }
```

- `size`: `[length, height, width]`, each 1..16: the columns, rows and slices of the picture.
- `palette`: 3 or 4 colours as `#rrggbb` (another count is a load error).
- `slices`: `width` slices; each is `height` strings of `length` characters, the side view:
  row 0 is the top, column 0 the **front** (the muzzle's end), slice 0 the left. `.` is
  empty, `1`..`4` a palette colour (a number past the palette is an error). A model with no
  voxel is an error.
- `grip`: `[column, row, slice]`, a voxel the hand closes round; it must be inside the size.
- Unknown fields, wrong types, rows of the wrong length and the rest are load errors naming
  the file, line and column (the loader's usual messages, with suggestions).

An item gets `"model": "<id>"` (a model of the registry; an unknown id is an error with a
suggestion). The base pack ships `data/models/weapons.json` with `pistol`, `rifle`,
`sniper` and `rocket_launcher`, and `model` on the four items. Silhouettes: the pistol a
short barrel over a slanted grip; the rifle a long barrel, a stock and a magazine; the
sniper a longer barrel, a scope on top and a stock; the rocket launcher a fat tube with a
wider front. 3 or 4 colours each (dark body, grey metal, light highlight, a brown for
wood). `Registry.models: []Model_Def` is hashed with the registry, sent with the packs and
hot-reloaded: a new model is data only.

## Names and files

- `common/game/models.odin`: `Model_Def {id, size, palette, colors, voxels, grip}`, the
  voxel index `x + z * size.x + y * size.x * size.z` (model space: +x right, +y up, -z
  front), `model_voxel`; `registry_models.odin`: `read_model`; `Item_Info.model:
  Maybe(u16)`, an index into `Registry.models`.
- `client/viewmodel_mesh.odin`: `Held_Vertex {pos, uv, color, layer}` and `Held_Mesh`;
  `held_mesh_model`, `held_mesh_icon`, `held_mesh_block`, `held_mesh_arm`, all pure (no
  GPU); `held_kind` and `held_mesh_for(reg, item)`, with a cache by item cleared when the
  registry changes. A face between two voxels is not made. Voxel faces carry a flat
  colour (`layer = HELD_FLAT`); a block's carry the texture layer and the uv the world
  shader derives from position and face.
- `client/viewmodel.odin`: `Vm_State`, `Vm_Event`, `vm_event`, `vm_update`, `vm_pose` (the
  kind's placement and the animation's offsets as one transform), the third-person grip
  (`avatar_hand`), the session's calls and the scope overlay.
- `client/render/held_pass.odin` with `assets/shaders/held.vert` and `held.frag`: vertices
  transformed on the CPU each frame (a few hundred) into a mapped buffer like the entity
  pass's; two draws: the view model (its own scene, depth cleared with `gpu.clear_depth`)
  and the third person's held items (the world's scene, depth tested).
- `client/passes.odin` gets `.Held`; `avatar_pose` takes a `Hold` (`.None`, `.Item`,
  `.Gun`) instead of a bool; `avatar_draw` draws the hand's item.

## Tests

- **A mesh for each held kind**: a cube block (dirt), shaped blocks (stairs, a slab, a
  fence), a flat item (a tool, a material), and each of the four weapons produce a
  non-empty mesh inside the size they promise; the base pack's models have 3 or 4 colours
  each; a face between two voxels is not made (a 2 x 1 x 1 model has 10 quads, not 12); a
  block's mesh equals `mesh_chunk`'s over the one-block snapshot, quad for quad.
- **Model data**: a pack adds a model and an item that holds it with no code change; broken
  models each fail with their exact message (an unknown palette number, a row too long, a
  grip outside, five colours, a missing slice, a zero-size, an unknown item model).
- **The projection**: the view model's matrix is the same for every `settings.fov`, with the
  sprint kick and with the zoom at its end; it changes with the aspect only.
- **Animation states follow the events**: a swing runs 0.28 s and ends; a place 0.22 s; a
  shot kicks and the kick decays; a reload dips for exactly its time and returns to rest;
  the sway and the bob are zero with "View bobbing" off and with Reduce motion on, and not
  zero with both clear; the sniper's model is hidden above the zoom threshold and back
  below it, other weapons never hidden.
- **Third person**: the arm holding a gun is level and forward (the hand's tip is in front
  of the shoulder), holding a block less raised, nothing at rest; the grip's transform
  puts each kind's grip at the hand.
- **F1** (`hud_hidden`) leaves the pass nothing to draw (a function of the state).
- **CI screenshots** (the existing Screenshots step): each weapon, a held block, a held
  shaped block and a held flat item, in first person; the same in third person (F5);
  named `held_<what>_first` and `held_<what>_third`; the owner reviews them.
- **Frame time** (`build bench`): the benchmark with a weapon in hand stays inside the
  budget the bench already states.
- The suite, `build check`, `build determinism`, the bandwidth roots (nothing is sent).

**Exit check (M48k):** the tests above pass, the whole suite and `build check`, CI green
with the screenshots uploaded.

## Decisions (not in the issue)

- The milestone is **M48k**, after M48f-M48j and ahead of M49, as the issue
  asks (first among the open rows).
- The model format is a **side-view picture with a thickness** (slices): the owner reviews
  and edits the data, and a gun reads best as a profile. The voxel pitch and the held
  placements are constants in code; a model is shape and colours, and its `grip`.
- The swing, place, recoil and reload motions play with "View bobbing" off: they are not
  bobbing. The issue limits only the sway and the bob.
- The view model is lit by the sky and the brightness setting, not by block light or fog:
  a lamp-lit cave does not darken it. No new lighting.
- The existing raised arm swung backwards (a pitch of -1.1 about the shoulder moves the
  hand toward +z, behind the avatar, whose front is -z); `avatar_pose` is fixed to raise it
  forward and its tests pin that.
- The rocket launcher (a tool) has a model, recoil and the aimed arm; it does not zoom and
  its use is the existing `session_fire`.
- Other players' held items come from `Player_Record.held`, already replicated.
- Block items are drawn at their default state, `info.block` as the item places it.
- The scope overlay is drawn from rectangles on the UI layer (a circle by scanlines).
