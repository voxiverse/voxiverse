# M49d: a compact universe creation screen and a new Ignite animation

Owner-approved (issue #63): it sits after M49 and M49b. Built exactly as the owner described and nothing more: no
colours, effects or layout beyond the text below. Where this spec had to decide something
the issue does not state, the decision is in "Decisions" below, with the principle it
follows. M48e's creation screen (`screen_creation.odin`) and Ignite (`screen_ignite.odin`)
are what this replaces; the seed, Roll, mode and universe-plan logic stay.

## The compact creation screen

One centred panel that fits a 1280 x 720 window at the default UI scale with **no
scrolling**: every control is visible without moving anything.

- **Top band:** the galaxy preview on the left and the rotating home planet on the right,
  side by side (the planet is `draw_planet`, turning as it does now).
- **Below, in this order:**
  1. the name field;
  2. the seed's glyphs, with the **Roll** button on the same row;
  3. **Creative / Survival** as one toggle;
  4. the **Ignite** button.
- All colours come from the theme (the interface) or from data (the galaxy's stars and the
  planet's palette: `star_color`, `home_planet_palette`, issue #62). **No literals.**

## The new Ignite animation

About 2.5 s, skippable by any key or click:

1. The galaxy's arms **wind inward** and the whole galaxy collapses to **a single bright
   point**.
2. A **flash**, and an **expanding shockwave ring** from that point.
3. **Stars streak outward** (the title screen's warp effect).
4. The camera **dives toward the home planet**, which grows from a dot until it fills the
   screen.
5. **Cross-fade into the loading screen.**

**Reduce motion:** all of it is replaced by a simple fade. The world loads during the
animation; if loading takes longer, the loading screen follows as usual (as now).

## Tests

- **Layout:** the panel's layout at 1280 x 720 and the default scale: every control's
  rect (galaxy, planet, name, seed, Roll, toggle, Ignite) is inside the screen and inside
  the panel, nothing overlaps, the order top to bottom is the one above, the galaxy is left
  of the planet, Roll shares the seed's row, and no scroll area is used (a function of the
  layout, not a pixel check).
- **Phases:** `ignite_phase(t)` gives the five phases in order (wind and collapse, flash
  and ring, streaks, dive, cross-fade), each starting where the last ends, the durations
  adding up to `IGNITE_SECONDS` (2.5, within 0.05); the collapse ends on one point (every
  star's position equals the centre), the ring's radius grows with time, the planet's
  radius grows from a dot to at least the screen's diagonal.
- **Skip:** a key or a click in a later frame ends it at once, and the app is on the loading
  screen (what `enter_world` set up) with the animation inactive; the frame that started it
  does not count.
- **Reduce motion:** the plan is a fade only: the duration is `IGNITE_FADE`, no star, ring
  or planet is drawn (the draw list holds one alpha rect).
- **No literals:** the creation and Ignite files hold no colour literal (a scan of
  `screen_creation.odin` and `screen_ignite.odin`, with no exemptions); a theme change moves
  every UI colour in the screen.
- **CI screenshots** (the Screenshots step): `screen_creation`, and mid-Ignite frames
  `ignite_wind`, `ignite_flash`, `ignite_streaks`, `ignite_dive` (a console way to freeze
  the animation at a time, as `camera` and `hold` do for pictures), for the owner's review.
- The suite, `build check`, `build determinism` and the bandwidth roots (nothing is sent).

**Exit check (M49d):** the tests above pass, the whole suite and `build check`, CI green
with the screenshots uploaded.

## Decisions (not in the issue)

- The milestone is **M49d** (M49c is the name M49's spec keeps for its own remainder), placed
  after M49b as the issue says, not first among the open rows: the issue's "not before the
  LAN party" outranks the generic rule that puts new work first.
- **The flash and the ring** use `theme.text` (the theme's brightest colour); the ground
  behind the animation is `theme.background`. Adding no theme colour follows "don't add
  colours".
- **The single bright point** is `theme.text`, as the flash; the stars keep their data colours.
- **The streaks** are the title screen's warp effect called with the animation's time, not
  a copy: if its draw is not a function of time yet, it is made one in this milestone.
- **Proportions** (the spec gives none): the panel is `min(screen.w - 40, 720)` wide, the
  top band is half the panel's width each (planet and galaxy square), the rows are
  `theme.row_height`; the title and the Cancel button stay (Cancel beside Ignite is not in
  the owner's list, so it moves to Esc, which every screen already has; no button is added).
- **Phase times** (the spec gives only the total): wind and collapse 0.8 s, flash and ring
  0.4 s (the ring continuing under the streaks), streaks 0.4 s, dive 0.6 s, cross-fade
  0.3 s: 2.5 s. Constants beside `IGNITE_SECONDS`.
- **The facts** (climate, sky, terrain) of M48e's screen leave: the owner's list does not
  have them, and without them nothing needs scrolling. Their strings stay in the table if
  something else uses them, else the string-table scan tells which to remove.
- **Unattended sessions:** the milestone is one session; if it must split, the first part
  is the creation screen and its layout test, the remainder the animation, with its own
  exit check.

## Check by hand

- Run the game at 1280 x 720 and open the creation screen: the galaxy is left, the planet
  right, name, seed with Roll, the mode toggle and Ignite below, nothing scrolls.
- Press Ignite and watch it: the galaxy winds in to a point, flash and ring, streaks, the
  dive to the planet, a fade into the loading screen; about 2.5 s. Press a key during it:
  loading at once. With Reduce motion on: only a fade.
