# M49d: a compact universe creation screen and a new Ignite animation

Owner-approved (issue #63). In the order of 2026-10-30 (#83) it comes after R15b and before
M54b. Built exactly as the owner described and nothing more: no colours, effects or layout
beyond the text below. Where this spec had to decide something the issue does not state, the
decision is in "Decisions" below, with the principle it follows; what only the owner can
decide is under "Open questions" and does not block. M48e's creation screen
(`client/screen_creation.odin`) and Ignite (`client/screen_ignite.odin`) are what this
replaces; the seed, Roll, mode and universe-plan logic stay.

## The compact creation screen

One centred panel that fits a 1280 x 720 window at the default UI scale with **no
scrolling**: every control is visible without moving anything.

- **Top band:** the galaxy preview on the left and the rotating home planet on the right,
  side by side (the planet is `draw_planet`, turning as it does now).
- **Below, in this order:**
  1. the name field;
  2. the seed's glyphs, with the **Roll** button on the same row;
  3. **Creative / Survival** as one toggle;
  4. the **Ignite** button.
- All colours come from the theme (the interface) or from data (the galaxy's stars and the
  planet's palette: `star_color`, `home_planet_palette`, issue #62). **No literals.**

### `creation_layout` (`client/screen_creation.odin`)

`creation_layout(screen: ui.Rect, t: ui.Theme) -> Creation_Layout`, a pure function:
`Creation_Layout {panel, title, galaxy, planet, name, seed, glyphs, roll, mode, ignite:
ui.Rect, fits: bool}`. `creation_screen` draws from it and nothing else places a control;
`fits` is false when the panel would need more height than the screen gives (the screen
then draws the same rows, clipped: there is no scroll area to fall back to, and the test
proves `fits` at every supported size).

## The new Ignite animation

About 2.5 s, skippable by any key or click:

1. The galaxy's arms **wind inward** and the whole galaxy collapses to **a single bright
   point**.
2. A **flash**, and an **expanding shockwave ring** from that point.
3. **Stars streak outward** (the title screen's warp effect).
4. The camera **dives toward the home planet**, which grows from a dot until it fills the
   screen.
5. **Cross-fade into the loading screen.**

**Reduce motion:** all of it is replaced by a simple fade. The world loads during the
animation; if loading takes longer, the loading screen follows as usual (as now).

### Design

- `Ignite_Phase :: enum {Wind, Flash, Streaks, Dive, Fade, Done}` and
  `ignite_phase(t: f32) -> (phase: Ignite_Phase, x: f32)` (`x` in 0..1 within the phase), from
  a constant table `IGNITE_PHASES` of durations (Decisions) summing to `IGNITE_SECONDS`.
- `ignite_plan(i: ^Ignite, screen: ui.Rect) -> Ignite_Frame`: everything a frame shows as
  numbers, computed without drawing: the stars' positions (wind: each star turned about the
  centre by an angle growing toward the middle, `angle = WIND_TURNS * 2π * e * (1 - r)`, and
  pulled in by `(1 - e)`; `e` eased), the point's alpha, the flash's alpha, the ring's radius,
  the streak speed factor, the planet's radius and the fade's alpha. `ignite_draw` draws an
  `Ignite_Frame`; the tests read the plan, not pixels.
- **Streaks:** Ignite owns a `Starfield` of its own (not the menus' one, so the title's stars
  are not disturbed), stepped by `starfield_update` with `dt * IGNITE_WARP_SPEED` during the
  streak phase (a speed parameter added to `starfield_update`; the title passes 1), drawn by
  `starfield_draw`. The ring keeps growing under the streaks.
- **Time freeze for pictures:** `set debug.ignite_at <seconds>` (a development variable, as
  `debug.*` are) holds an active Ignite at that time (stepping stops; skipping still works);
  `creation seed <n>` (a console command) sets the creation screen's seed as if typed; and
  `ignite show` (development) starts Ignite with the creation screen's stars and planet
  without `enter_world`, so a scene makes no universe on disk.
- **Scenes at the screen's size.** Scenes render at `SCENE_SIZE` (640 x 360,
  `client/scenes.odin`), where the compact screen does not fit (it is made for 1280 x 720 UI
  units). A scene line may start with a size (`creation: @1280x720 screen worlds create; ...`),
  parsed by `scene_find`; the default stays 640 x 360, so no accepted picture moves. The
  menus' starfield is one more fixed condition of a scene (a fixed seed, stepped with
  `dt = 0`), and Ignite's own starfield is seeded from the universe's seed.

4. `creation seed`, `ignite show`, `debug.ignite_at`, sized scenes, the scenes, accepted (`build images --accept`) after

   section of `docs/debugging.md`: the two console additions); history.md.

## Tests

All in `client/creation_test.odin` unless named; no test reads a clock (T5).

- `test_creation_layout_fits`: for every 16:9 window from 1280 x 720 to 3840 x 2160 in 160 px
  steps, and 1280 x 800 and 1366 x 768, at the automatic `ui_scale` and the default theme:
  `fits`; every control's rect (galaxy, planet, name, seed, glyphs, Roll, mode, Ignite) inside
  the screen and the panel; no two overlap; top to bottom name < seed row < mode < Ignite, all
  below the top band; the galaxy left of the planet with equal heights; Roll's and the seed's
  rows the same `y`; the panel centred within one unit.
- `test_creation_draws_no_scroll`: one `ui_frame` of the screen at 1280 x 720 draws no
  scroll bar and pushes no clip narrower than the panel (the draw list's clip commands).
- `test_creation_mode_toggle`: one click on the toggle goes Creative → Survival → Creative;
  Left and Right while focused do the same (it is `ui.choice` with the two mode names); the
  mode reaches `universe_plan`.
- `test_ignite_phases`: `ignite_phase` gives the five phases in order over `t` in 1 ms steps,
  each starting where the last ends, `x` from 0 to 1 within each, `Done` from
  `IGNITE_SECONDS`; the table sums to 2.5 (within 0.05).
- `test_ignite_plan`: at the end of Wind every star's position equals the centre (within
  1e-4 of the screen); Wind turns stars (a star's angle about the centre at `x = 0.5` differs
  from its start); the ring's radius increases strictly through Flash and Streaks; the streak
  speed factor is above 1 only in Streaks; the planet's radius starts under 8 px and ends at or
  above the screen's half diagonal; the fade's alpha goes 0 → 1 over Fade.
- `test_ignite_plays_and_skips` (reworked): 2.5 s of 60 Hz frames, the cue once; a key or a
  click after the first frame ends it with `app.screen` and the session as `enter_world` set
  them and `ignite.active` false; the first frame's input does not skip.
- `test_ignite_reduce_motion`: the duration is `IGNITE_FADE`; every frame's plan has no star,
  ring, streak or planet, and the draw list holds exactly one rect.
- `test_creation_colours_from_theme_or_data`: the screen and one frame of each Ignite phase
  drawn with a theme whose every colour field is a distinct sentinel; every vertex colour of
  the output (alpha aside, as opacity and fades scale it) is a theme colour, a `star_color`
  of the galaxy's stars, a `STAR_TINTS` entry or a lit shade of the planet's palette
  (`draw_planet`'s `lit` applied to one of its four colours); a second sentinel theme changes
  every interface-coloured vertex.
- `tools/build/lints_test.odin`: the widened `lint_colour_literals` finds `{0, 0, 0, 255}` and
  `{255, 255, 255, u8(a)}` as an argument in a layer file and not in a comment or a non-layer
  file; `check` passes over the tree with no exemption.
- `client/scenes_test.odin` (or beside `scene_find`'s tests): a sized line parses to its
  size and script; a line without one keeps `SCENE_SIZE`; a malformed size is an error naming
  the line.
- **Scenes** (`assets/baselines/images/scenes.txt`, `build images` in CI, all
  `@1280x720`): `creation` (`screen worlds create; creation seed 42`), `ignite_wind`,
  `ignite_flash`, `ignite_streaks`, `ignite_dive` (the same, then `set debug.ignite_at` 0.5,
  0.95, 1.35 or 1.8 and `ignite show`). Each is rendered twice in the session and compared
  with itself before it is accepted (the repeatability `build images` relies on).
- The suite, `build check`, `build determinism` and the bandwidth baselines unchanged (nothing
  is sent).

## Decisions (not in the issue)

- The milestone is **M49d** (M49c is the name M49's spec keeps for its own remainder). Its
  place is the order of 2026-10-30 (#83): after R15b, before M54b.
- **The flash, the point and the ring** use `theme.text` (the theme's brightest colour); the
  ground behind the animation is `theme.background`. Adding no theme colour follows "don't
  add colours". The stars keep their data colours.
- **The streaks** are the title screen's warp (`starfield.odin`) with a speed parameter, not
  a copy.
- **Proportions** (the spec gives none): the panel is `min(screen.w - 40, 720)` wide, the
  top band is half the panel's width each (planet and galaxy square), the rows are
  `theme.row_height`; the title stays. Cancel is not in the owner's list: it moves to Esc,
  which every screen already has; no button is added.
- **The seed row** keeps the typed seed field (the seed logic stays: "typed text or a number
  becomes a seed"): the field, then the glyph address, then Roll (90 px), on one row.
- **The toggle** is `ui.choice` over the two modes (the engine's widget; no new one).
- **Phase times** (the spec gives only the total): wind 0.8 s, flash 0.4 s (the ring
  continuing under the streaks), streaks 0.4 s, dive 0.6 s, fade 0.3 s: 2.5 s. `WIND_TURNS`
  1.5, `IGNITE_WARP_SPEED` 12. Constants beside `IGNITE_SECONDS`.
- **The facts** (climate, sky, terrain) of M48e's screen leave: the owner's list does not
  have them, and without them nothing needs scrolling. Their strings go if the string-table
  scan finds no other user.
- **Unattended sessions:** the milestone is one session; if it must split, the first part is
  the screen, its layout and colour tests and the `creation` scene; the remainder the
  animation, with the phase, plan, skip and Reduce motion tests and the four Ignite scenes as
  its exit check.
