# M50: entity framework v2, part one (collision, the archetype store, the spatial query service)

**Why.** Entities are a fixed struct with every kind's fields in it (`server/entities.odin`'s
`Entity`: body, brain, stack, shorn, genes, growth, love, wait, age, owner, action), kept in
one `[dynamic]Entity` of at most `MAX_ENTITIES` 512, found by a linear `entity_find`,
walked by a `switch` on a closed `game.Entity_Kind` enum (Sheep, Rocket, Item). A pack
cannot add a kind, a script cannot add data to one, and every "who is near" is a loop over
all entities and all clients. The survey (checked 2026-10-30, #85):

- Loops over `w.entities.list` outside the entity file's own tick: `/summon`'s cap
  (`admin.odin`), a chest's restore (`block_entities.odin`, two), `/kill`
  (`commands_world.odin`), the ledger's sums (`ledger.odin`, two), the journal's lowest live
  action (`journal.odin`), merging (`items.odin`), lag compensation's frame
  (`rewind.odin`), hitscan (`weapons.odin`), saving (`save.odin`), the hash
  (`state_hash.odin`) and `perturb` (`stepped.odin`); inside `entities.odin`: `mate_for`,
  `explode`, rocket hits, `entity_find`, `entities_put`, the spawn cap. Players near
  something: `nearest_player` and `anyone_within` loop over `s.clients`, and so do pickup,
  rocket hits, explosions, hitscan and the rewind frame. None of it has a spatial index.
- All entities stay in memory forever; one whose chunk is not ready is frozen
  (`chunk_ready`). The save is one `entities.cfg` per world, a text line per entity
  (`save.odin`: `write_entities`, `load_entities`).
- Movement is `game.body_step` (`common/game/entity.odin`) over `sweep`
  (`common/game/physics.odin`), the axis-by-axis sweep players use, in the order y, x, z.
  Players also step up (`STEP_HEIGHT` 0.5625, the private `try_step_up`: up, across, down by
  what it rose, kept only if it landed and went strictly farther by `|dx| + |dz|`); sheep do
  not (they jump). Rockets are a ray from a point (`rocket_step`), not a box.
- Players are not entities: `Client.player` (`game.Player_State`) is the truth, replicated as
  `Player_Motion`.
- The client fix of the old step 1 is done (#27: `track_hold`, `STALE_SECONDS`,
  `client/still_test.odin`'s `test_still_player_stays_visible` and
  `test_still_sheep_stays_visible`); the "drops a stack" test is still due and is here.

**The split (decided 2026-10-30, #85; the old M50 plus M50b split was still two sessions'
worth each).** Entities v2 is four rows, each with its own exit check:

- **M50** (this row): the libraries, with no change to what the game does: shared collision
  (`collide_move`, `step_up`, `cast_box`, a box source for M50c), the archetype store and the
  spatial query service, each with its differential and property tests and its benchmark;
  bodies moved onto `collide_move` with identical results.
- **[M50e](M50e.md):** kinds as data (the `entity` registry kind, which M47 did not make),
  the components, the systems, sheep, items and rockets on the store, players as entities,
  every loop above moved onto the spatial service; the 2 000-entity tick budget.
- **[M50f](M50f.md):** persistence and handoff (entities belong to chunks, unload and load
  with them, saved in M46's region chunk records; `entities.cfg` goes); memory flat over
  churn.
- **[M50b](M50b.md):** replication v2 (quantized motion, rates by distance, the jitter
  buffer) and the bandwidth and loss tests.

**What M50c and M50d need from entities v2 (checked against their specs, 2026-10-30, #85).**
A moving block structure is an entity of kind `voxiverse:structure` with `Transform` and
`Body` components, riders, and its cells in `Block_Query`. So:

- The name `Body` is M50c's (the structure's grid and motion). Entities v2's physics
  component is **`Physics`** (`velocity`, `on_ground`), not `Body` (M50e).
- Components with variable-size data: a component is a fixed-size value; one that needs more
  (M50c's grid, block entities and riders) holds a handle into a pool its system owns, and the
  store calls the component's `release` when the component leaves an entity (removed, or the
  entity despawned or unloaded), so nothing leaks (M50, the store's `Component_Info`).
- Components are registered at run time (`store_init` takes the table), so M50c (and M52's
  scripts) add components without editing an enum in `common/game`.
- Collision consults a **box source** besides the cells (`Block_Query.extra`, below), so
  M50c can put bodies' cells into players' and entities' movement without touching
  `sweep`'s callers. Nil costs nothing and changes nothing.
- The box source's boxes are axis-aligned (`sweep` and `cast_box` work per axis). A body
  turned by a yaw that is not a multiple of 90 degrees gives each of its cells' collision
  boxes as the box's axis-aligned bounds in world space (conservative: nobody falls through
  or passes into a turning body; exact at rest on a right angle). This is M50c's to build
  and its spec says so; M50 only guarantees that a source's boxes collide exactly like cells'.
- Spatial entries are boxes (`lo`, `hi`), not a width and height, so a structure's bounds
  fit; `Spatial_Flags` is a `bit_set` M50c extends (`Structure`).
- Players stay `Client.player`; a system that moves a player (M50c's riding and pushing)
  writes `Client.player` through `Player_Link` (M50e), and `player_entity_sync` copies it to
  `Transform`.
- The systems' entry point keeps the name `entities_tick(w)` (M50e), so M50c's
  `bodies_tick` "right after `entities_tick`" stays true.
- M50f's chunk-record `entities` section and its `release`/encode hooks are what M50d saves
  bodies through.

## Design (M50)

### Collision (`common/game/collide.odin`; `physics.odin` keeps `sweep`)

- **The box source.** `Block_Query` gains `extra: Box_Source` (`Box_Source :: struct {data:
  rawptr, boxes: proc(data: rawptr, lo, hi: [3]f64, out: ^[dynamic]World_Box)}`,
  `World_Box :: struct {lo, hi: [3]f64}`). `sweep`, `box_blocked`, `supported` and
  `cast_box` call it once per call with the bounds they search (the swept box) and treat each
  box it gives like a cell's collision box. With `boxes == nil` they do exactly what they do
  today (the M38 roots and every physics test unchanged; a test runs every physics test
  file's cases with a source that gives no boxes and compares bit for bit).
- **`step_up`.** Today's private `try_step_up` becomes `step_up(q, position, half_width,
  height, from, delta: [3]f64, vel: [3]f32, step: f64) -> (Step_Result, bool)`, public and
  unchanged in arithmetic and order (`STEP_HEIGHT` passed by `player_step`).
- **`collide_move(q, position: [3]f64, half_width, height: f64, delta: [3]f64, velocity:
  [3]f32, step: f64) -> Collide {position: [3]f64, velocity: [3]f32, hit: [3]bool,
  on_ground, stepped: bool}`:** `body_step`'s loop (y, x, z through `sweep`, velocity zeroed
  on a hit axis, `on_ground` on a downward y hit), then, with `step > 0`, when it was on the
  ground and stopped on x or z, `step_up`. `body_step` calls it with step 0: identical
  results. `player_step` keeps its own loop (the sneak edge guard runs between its axes) and
  calls `step_up`, so there is one step-up in the code.
- **`cast_box(q, lo, hi: [3]f64, delta: [3]f64) -> (fraction: f64, axis: int, hit:
  bool)`:** the earliest time of impact in [0, 1] of the box moving by `delta` against the
  collision boxes (cells and `extra`) in its swept bounds: per box a slab test on the
  Minkowski sum, ties to the lowest axis, a box already overlapped at the start ignored (as
  `sweep` ignores a face it starts inside). Used by projectiles from M50e.
- `ray_box` and `entity_box` stay in `common/game/entity.odin` until M50e replaces kinds.

### The archetype store (`common/game/archetype.odin`, no server imports)

- `Component :: distinct u8` (0..31), `Component_Set :: bit_set[0 ..< 32; u32]`.
  `Component_Info :: struct {name: string, type: typeid, size, align: int, release:
  proc(owner: rawptr, value: rawptr) (nil: none)}`. `store_init(st, infos: []Component_Info,
  owner: rawptr, max_entities: int)`; the table is fixed for the store's life.
- An **archetype** is a `Component_Set` with one table: `ids: []Entity_Id` and one column per
  component it has. Each column (and `ids`) is its own virtual reservation of `max_entities *
  size` (`core:mem/virtual`'s `reserve` and `commit`, as `Block_Arrays` does in `common/game/chunk.odin`), committed in 64-row steps and never
  shrunk: rows never move except by swap-remove, and committed memory stops at the high-water
  mark (flat over churn). At most `MAX_ARCHETYPES :: 64` a store, found through `by_set` (a
  fixed map, lookup only), kept in creation order.
- `Entity_Id :: distinct u32` (the id on the wire, in commands, logs and saves; from the
  store's `next_id`, never reused; at `max(u32)` spawning refuses with a log line).
  `Entity_Handle` is a `base.Pool` handle (generation-checked). `Entity_Store {slots:
  base.Pool(Entity_Slot, Entity_Handle), by_id (fixed map, lookup only), archetypes, by_set,
  commands, next_id, infos, owner}`; `Entity_Slot {id, archetype: u16, row: u32}`.
- API: `store_spawn(st, set) -> (Entity_Id, Entity_Handle)` (deferred: below),
  `store_spawn_with_id` (M50f's load), `store_despawn(st, h)`, `store_add(st, h, c, value:
  ^$T)`, `store_remove(st, h, c)`, `store_get(st, h, c, $T) -> ^T` (nil if absent; in
  debug, asserts `infos[c].type == T`), `store_find(st, id) -> (Entity_Handle, bool)`,
  `store_query(st, all, none: Component_Set) -> Query_Iter` yielding `(^Archetype, rows:
  int)`, `column(arch, c, $T) -> []T`, `store_apply(st)`, `store_count(st)`,
  `store_committed_bytes(st)`. Adding or removing a component moves the entity to the
  archetype of its new set (appended there, swap-removed from the old).
- **Structural changes are deferred**: spawns, despawns, adds and removes go into
  `commands` (a fixed ring of `max_entities * 2`; full is a returned error, never silent) and
  `store_apply` runs them in issue order. A spawned entity is seen by the next query after
  the apply. `release` runs in `store_apply` for each component that leaves.
- **Determinism (M38):** archetypes in creation order, rows in row order, swap-remove,
  commands in issue order; no map is iterated (a lint test: `archetype.odin` has no `for ...
  in` over a map). `store_hash(st, k)` folds `next_id`, then per archetype its set and count,
  then each row's id and its components in component order, each through M38's `fold` with
  the path `entities[archetype 2].<Component name>[5].<field>`.

### The spatial query service (`common/game/spatial.odin`; the server's entities and, from M50b, the client's)

- A grid of chunk-sized cells (16³): `cells: map[Chunk_Pos]u32` (fixed, lookup only), per
  cell an intrusive doubly linked list through the entries' `next`/`prev`; entries indexed
  like the store's slots: `Spatial_Entry {id: Entity_Id, lo, hi: [3]f64, kind: u16, flags:
  Spatial_Flags}`, `Spatial_Flags :: bit_set[Spatial_Flag; u16]`, `Spatial_Flag :: enum u8
  {Hittable, Player, Item, Natural, Saved, Frozen}` (room for M50c's `Structure`). An entry is
  bucketed by its box's bottom centre. Insert, move (re-bucketing only when the cell changes)
  and remove are O(1); empty cells leave the map. Queries search the cells the query's bounds
  overlap, widened by `SPATIAL_MAX_EXTENT :: 4` blocks (a box larger than that is refused at
  insert unless flagged `Large`, which joins a separate list every query scans: M50c's
  structures).
- Queries, all returning ids **ascending** (so no result depends on insertion order or the
  map; `nearest` breaks ties by id), filtered by `Spatial_Filter {kind: u16 (0: any), all,
  none: Spatial_Flags, exclude: Entity_Id}`, results appended to a caller's buffer (the
  temp allocator in systems):
  - `spatial_sphere(sp, center, radius, filter, out)` (entries whose bottom centre is within);
  - `spatial_cylinder(sp, center, radius, half_height, filter, out)` (horizontal distance and
    vertical extent: the spawn cap's and `ENTITY_VIEW`'s shape);
  - `spatial_box(sp, lo, hi, filter, out)` (boxes overlapping);
  - `spatial_nearest(sp, center, radius, filter) -> (Entity_Id, f64, bool)`;
  - `spatial_count(sp, center, radius, filter) -> int`;
  - `spatial_cast(sp, origin, dir: [3]f64, max, half: f64, filter) -> (Entity_Id, f64, bool)`
    (the nearest entry box that a ray, or a box of half size `half` moving along it, enters:
    a 3D DDA over the cells along it, with `ray_box` per entry);
  - `spatial_chunk(sp, pos, out)` (the entries bucketed in a chunk: M50f).
- Blocks: `cells_find(q, lo, hi, m: ^Matcher, out, max_volume := 32_768) -> int` and
  `cells_nearest(q, center, radius, m) -> (Block_Pos, bool)` over a `Block_Query`, in (y, z,
  x) order, `m` M49b's compiled matcher (shared). Refused past `max_volume` (returns -1, a
  caller bug: asserted).

## Tests (M50)

- `client/still_test.odin`, `test_dropped_stack_stays_visible`: a stepped server and a
  session; a player drops a stack on flat ground; 5 s (150 ticks) later, with no snapshot
  record for it since it settled, the client still draws it at its resting place (within
  1/64 block).
- `common/game/collide_test.odin`:
  - `test_collide_matches_body_step`: 10 000 random boxes, deltas and velocities (seeded
    `base.Rng`, seed 50) in a random field of every M48 shape: `collide_move` with step 0
    gives bit-identical positions, velocities and `on_ground` to `body_step`'s old loop
    (copied into the test as the reference; the comment says why).
  - `test_step_up_matches_player`: the same field, 10 000 player moves: `player_step` before
    and after the change (the old `try_step_up` copied as the reference) give bit-identical
    states.
  - `test_step_up_heights`: a box with `step` 0.6 climbs a slab (0.5) and a stair, not a full
    block; with `step` 0 it climbs neither.
  - `test_cast_box`: a 0.3 box at 32 blocks a second for one tick through a one-block wall
    is stopped at the wall's face (fraction within 1e-9 of the exact one) where the start and
    end test would miss it; a box starting inside a cell box is not stopped by it; 10 000
    random casts agree with a brute-force reference (the box stepped in 1/1000 of the delta,
    the first overlapping step) to within 1/1000.
  - `test_box_source`: a source giving one floating box: a falling body lands on it, a player
    walks into it and stops, `cast_box` hits it; a source giving no boxes leaves every case
    of `physics_test.odin`, `physics_shapes_test.odin` and the two differential tests above
    bit-identical.
- `common/game/archetype_test.odin`:
  - `test_store_model`: 100 000 random operations (spawn with random sets, despawn, add,
    remove, get, query, apply at random points) against a plain reference model (a map from
    id to a struct of optional values): after every apply, every entity's components equal
    the model's, every query yields exactly the model's entities in archetype creation order
    then row order, ids are never reused.
  - `test_store_deferred`: commands apply in issue order; a despawn then an add to the same
    entity in one batch is refused (returned), not applied to a dead slot; the ring full is
    an error.
  - `test_store_release`: `release` runs once per component leaving, on remove, despawn and
    `store_destroy`, never twice (a counter per value).
  - `test_store_flat_over_churn`: 20 rounds of spawning 2 000 entities across 8 archetypes and
    despawning them all: `store_committed_bytes` after round 2 equals after round 20.
  - `test_store_hash_order`: two stores given the same operations in the same order hash
    alike; the same entities built in a different order hash differently (the hash sees row
    order, as M38 wants); a perturbed value names its path.
- `common/game/spatial_test.odin`:
  - `test_spatial_brute_force`: 2 000 random entries (random boxes up to 4 blocks, random
    flags and kinds) and 10 000 random queries of every kind: every result equals a brute
    force over all entries, ascending; after 10 000 random moves (many across cell borders)
    and removes, the same holds.
  - `test_spatial_insert_order`: the same entries inserted in three orders give identical
    results for every query.
  - `test_spatial_nearest_ties`: equal distances return the lowest id.
  - `test_spatial_large`: a `Large` entry of 32 blocks is found by queries far from its
    bottom centre; a non-`Large` box over 4 blocks is refused at insert.
  - `test_spatial_flat`: 20 rounds of 2 000 inserts and removes: the map's and entries'
    committed bytes after round 2 equal after round 20.
  - `test_cells_find`: `cells_find` and `cells_nearest` against brute force over a random
    world for a block, a state, a tag, a flag and a list matcher; (y, z, x) order; the
    volume cap refused.
- Unchanged in every line and passing: `server/entity_test.odin`, `sheep_test.odin`,
  `breeding_test.odin`, `items_test.odin`, `spawn_test.odin`, `common/game/physics_test.odin`,
  `physics_shapes_test.odin`, `determinism_test.odin`, the bandwidth tests (roots and bytes
  exactly the baseline's).

## Benchmarks (M50)

In `server/budgets.odin` (they need no server state, but the `budgets` scenario is where
budgets are judged), by `build bench budgets -check`: each metric a `base.Metric` with its
`METRIC_INFO` row (`base/metrics.odin`), its limit a row of `BUDGET_LIMITS`
(`tools/bench/budgets.odin`, `BUDGETS_VERSION` raised), judged on the median of the
repetitions as the existing budgets are. Each scene also returns what it did (operations
applied, rows visited, entries found), and `budgets_run` fails the run when that is not what
the scene asked for, so a scene that silently does less cannot pass:

| Scene / metric | What | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| `budget_store`: `Store_Op` | CPU ns per operation over 200 000 random spawn, add, remove and despawn operations with applies every 64 | 600 |
| `budget_store`: `Store_Iterate` | CPU ns per row of a two-component query over 4 096 entities in 8 archetypes | 15 |
| `budget_spatial`: `Spatial_Move` | CPU ns per move of 2 000 entries random-walking 0.2 blocks a step | 250 |
| `budget_spatial`: `Spatial_Sphere` | CPU ns per `spatial_sphere` of radius 16 among 2 000 entries over 128 x 128 blocks | 3 000 |
| `budget_collide`: `Collide_Move` | CPU ns per `collide_move` of a sheep-sized box with a 0.15-block delta in a field of M48 shapes | 2 000 |

  the old code; the bandwidth roots and bytes are the baseline's and the listed test files are

- `build bench budgets -check` within the five limits above.
