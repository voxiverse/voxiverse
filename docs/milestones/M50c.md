# M50c: moving block structures, part one (the body on the server: capture, kinematic motion, collision, pushing and riding)

**Why.** The owner's decision of 2026-10-30 (#83): groups of blocks that move and collide as
one body (doors, lifts, vehicles), scriptable, server-authoritative and replicated like
entities. This is the motion bedrock: **kinematic** bodies moved by scripts and data, never a
general rigid-body simulation (no mass, no forces, no stacking of bodies). This row builds the
body on the server, with tests calling Odin directly; [M50d](M50d.md) replicates, draws,
predicts riding, saves and exposes it to scripts.

**What this builds on:** M50, M50e and M50f as built (M50: the archetype store with
components registered at run time and their `release`, `Entity_Handle`, `Entity_Id`,
`common/game/collide.odin`'s `collide_move`, `step_up` and `cast_box` over `sweep`, the
`Block_Query.extra` box source, `common/game/spatial.odin` with `Large` entries; M50e:
`Kind_Id`, the components, `entities_tick`, players as entities through `Player_Link`, the
physics component named `Physics` so `Body` is this row's; M50f: the chunk record's
`entities` section with a component's `encode`/`decode`); M50b (quantized motion, the jitter buffer: used by
M50d); the shared physics (`common/game/physics.odin`: `player_step`, `Block_Query`,
`box_blocked`, `cell_boxes`, `STEP_HEIGHT`); block storage (`common/game/chunk.odin`'s
`Chunk_Blocks`, `Block_Arrays`), block entities (`common/game/block_entity.odin`), the
journal (`set_block`, `bulk_edit`, `Act`, `admit`, `Edit_System`), `base.Pool`. The session
first checks M50's actual names and corrects this spec in the same commit.

**The design (engineering, decided 2026-10-30):**

- **A body is an entity** of the built-in kind `voxiverse:structure` (the base pack's namespace is `voxiverse`, `BASE_NAMESPACE`) with components
  `Transform` (position as `[3]f64` and a yaw), `Body` (below) and `Origin`. It has an
  `Entity_Id` and handle like any entity, so interest, replication (M50d), the spatial
  query service and scripts treat it as one.
- **Its blocks are its own grid**, not the world's: `Body {size: [3]u8, blocks:
  []game.Block (size.x*size.y*size.z, local x fastest), entities: [dynamic]Block_Entity
  (re-keyed to the body's local index), pivot: [3]f32, velocity: [3]f32, yaw_rate: f32,
  riders: [dynamic]Entity_Id, state}`. While a block is in a body it is not in any chunk.
- **Capture and release.** `body_capture(w, box, pivot, act) -> (Entity_Handle,
  Capture_Error)` moves every non-air block of the box (and its block entities) out of the
  world into a new body in one journaled action (`Edit_System.Body`, appended), leaving air.
  `body_release(w, h, act) -> Release_Error` puts them back at the body's current pose
  rounded to the grid: allowed only when the yaw is a multiple of 90 degrees (within 0.01
  degrees, snapped) and every target cell is air or replaceable; otherwise refused and the
  body stays. Capture refuses a box with a block M47 marks unbreakable, a protected block
  (`admit`), a box over the ceilings, or a box with no block. Liquids in a captured box
  become still blocks of the body (no flow while moving); a released body's water flows
  again by the normal scheduled updates.
- **Motion is kinematic.** Each tick a body moves by its velocity and yaw rate (set by its
  driver: a script in M50d, a test here) or along a path (`body_move_to(h, position, yaw,
  ticks)`: linear in position and yaw over whole ticks). Rotation is about the vertical axis
  through `pivot` only (doors, turning vehicles); pitch and roll are not built (open question
  1). Positions and yaw are state: deterministic (`base.sin`/`base.cos`, the numbers lint).
- **Collision with the world.** A body's cells are boxes (each cell's `cell_boxes`, yawed).
  Each tick the move is cut into substeps, each at most 0.25 block of travel at any cell and
  at most 5 degrees of yaw. At each substep the body's cells are tested against the world's
  solid cells around them (the yawed cell box against axis-aligned cell boxes by separating
  axes in x-z plus the y interval). A substep that would overlap the world is not taken: the
  body stops at the previous one for this tick, its velocity is kept, and `blocked` is set
  (M50d tells the script). Bodies do not collide with each other in this row: two bodies
  that would overlap are both stopped at the substep before (the same rule), so they never
  interpenetrate.
- **Pushing and riding.** Players and entities are the movers the body meets:
  - A player or entity whose box overlaps the body after a substep is pushed out along the
    smallest separating direction if the space it is pushed into is free of world blocks
    (`box_blocked`); if not, the body's substep is not taken (blocked). Nobody is ever left
    inside a body and nobody is hurt by one: damage is a script's decision.
  - **Riding:** a player or entity standing on a body (its feet within `1/16` block above a
    body cell's top, the same ground test `player_step` uses) is a rider: each substep it is
    carried by the body's motion at its feet (translation plus the yaw about the pivot, its
    own yaw turned by the same amount), then its own movement applies. Riders are recomputed
    every tick and kept sorted by id.
  - **Players moving against bodies.** `player_step` and the entity movers collide with
    bodies as with blocks: the body source is M50's `Block_Query.extra` box source
    (`Body_Query`: the bodies whose bounds touch the mover's swept box, from the spatial
    query service, their cells as yawed boxes given as their axis-aligned bounds, as M50
    says). Server only in this row; M50d gives the client the same query for
    prediction.
- **Ceilings** (engine, sized from the tick budget; see the benchmark): a body's box at most
  32x32x32 cells and 4 096 blocks; 64 bodies a world; 16 riders a body. Over: refused with a
  reason. Whether these are also the creators' limits is open question 2.

**Files:** `common/game/body.odin` (the `Body` value, the yawed-cell overlap test, substep
planning, the rider test: shared, because M50d's client prediction runs the same code),
`server/bodies.odin` (`body_capture`, `body_release`, `body_move_to`, `bodies_tick` in the
world loop right after `entities_tick`, pushing, riding), `common/game/physics.odin`
(`Block_Query`'s body source).

**Order of the steps:**

1. `common/game/body.odin`: the grid, yawed overlap, substeps; unit tests and the fuzz.
2. Capture and release with the journal and `admit`; block entities carried.
3. `bodies_tick`: motion, world collision, blocked.
4. Pushing, riding, players and entities colliding with bodies on the server.
5. Benchmark scene.

**Tests** (`common/game/body_test.odin`, `server/bodies_test.odin`, on the harness):

- Capture then release with no motion: the world's chunks byte-identical to before
  (`test_capture_release_exact`), block entities (a filled chest) back with their contents,
  the ledger audit balanced, the journal holding one action each way; rollback by cause
  restores the pre-capture hash.
- Release at 90, 180, 270 degrees: blocks rotated (stairs and doors' facing states rotated
  by M48's `rotate`), a chest with its contents; release at 45 degrees refused; release into
  stone refused.
- A body moved into a wall stops at the last free substep, `blocked` set; a body moving 10
  blocks a tick still cannot pass through a one-block wall (substeps).
- A lift (a 3x1x3 platform) carries a standing player up 20 blocks and down with no fall
  damage and no correction on the server's own replay; a door (a 1x2x1 panel) swinging 90
  degrees about its edge pushes a player in its way out, and stops when the player is
  against a wall.
- Determinism: a scene of four bodies, eight riders and pushes, played twice and at both
  optimization levels, gives the same M38 root (`build determinism` scenario extended).
- Fuzz (`test_body_fuzz`, fixed seed, 20 000 cases): random body shapes, poses, velocities,
  yaw rates and player boxes; asserted: no mover ends a tick overlapping a body or the world,
  no body ends overlapping the world, riders stay on unless the body moves out from under
  them.
- Ceilings refused with their reasons.

**Benchmark.** `budget_bodies` in `server/budgets.odin`, judged by `build bench budgets
-check`: 64 bodies of 4 096 blocks each, half moving and turning, 16 riders on each, 300
ticks; limit: `bodies_tick`'s p99 at most 2 ms of CPU (a sixteenth of the tick period).

**Open questions for the owner** (none blocks this row):

1. Should bodies tilt (pitch and roll: ramps, rocking boats, aircraft), or is turning about
   the vertical axis enough?
2. Are the engine ceilings (32 blocks a side, 4 096 blocks, 64 bodies a world) also the
   creators' limits?
3. Should players (not only scripts) be able to make a structure in the game, and how
   (a block, a tool, the edit mode of M54d)?
4. Should bodies collide with one another and push each other (stacked lifts, a vehicle
   pushing another), beyond stopping before they overlap?

**Left out:** replication, drawing, the client's prediction, scripts, saves (M50d); forces,
gravity on bodies, mass, bodies falling; bodies crossing worlds or gates; light emitted by a
body's blocks while it moves (released blocks light as before).

**Exit check:** the tests above and the fuzz pass; `build bench budgets -check` within the
bodies' limit; the suite, `build check`, `build determinism`; CI green.

**Check by hand:** none (nothing is drawn yet).
