# M50d: moving block structures, part two (replication, drawing, riding prediction, scripts, saves and the journal)

**Why.** M50c built bodies on the server. Players must see them, ride them without
corrections, and creators must drive them from scripts; bodies must survive a save and be
undone by cause.

**What this builds on:** M50c (`Body`, `body_capture`, `body_release`, `body_move_to`,
`bodies_tick`, `Body_Query`, `common/game/body.odin`), M50b and M50g (quantized `Entity_Motion`,
rates by distance, the jitter buffer in `client/interp`, `Remote_Clock`), M39's state
kinds and lanes, the client's prediction (`common/game/prediction.odin`: `prediction_tick`,
`prediction_reconcile`), meshing (`client/mesh/mesh.odin`: `mesh_chunk` over a padded
`Snapshot`; `client/viewmodel_mesh.odin`'s one-block snapshot pattern), the held pass
(`client/render/held_pass.odin`: `held_world_begin(d, base)`, `held_world_add`,
`held_draw_world`, a mesh drawn at an `f64` origin), M52/M52b (capabilities, bindings, events,
journal entry kinds, snapshots in the save), M46's save transaction and region chunk records
(M50f moved entities into them, with a component's `encode`/`decode` for a body's grid).

**Replication.**

- A body's grid is sent once when it enters a client's interest, as a reliable record
  `Body_Shape {entity, size, pivot, blocks (palette + runs like chunks), revision}`, and again
  only when its revision changes (a script may swap blocks of a body, below). Its motion is
  M50b's `Entity_Motion` (position, yaw) at the snapshot lane's rate for its distance, plus a
  `Body_Motion` record (velocity, yaw rate, `blocked`, the path's end and end tick when
  moving by `body_move_to`), so the client can extrapolate exactly between snapshots.
- `PROTOCOL_VERSION` goes up; the dissector and netdump learn the records; the bandwidth
  baseline gains a `bodies` scene (four bodies moving in view of two players) and is
  re-accepted.

**Drawing.** A body's mesh is built once per revision by `mesh_chunk` over a snapshot of its
grid (a body is at most 32 cells a side: meshed as up to eight 16³ pieces), lit with the
light at its pivot's cell (no light through or from its blocks while moving: M50c), and
drawn by the held pass's world path at the body's interpolated position, with its yaw
applied (a model matrix added to `Held_Push`; `held.vert` gains the rotation). Bodies are
culled by their bounds against the frustum on the CPU (64 a world at most). R15 later moves
them with the other entities onto the GPU.

**Riding without corrections.** The client predicts its own player against bodies as the
server simulates it: `prediction_tick` runs `player_step` with a `Body_Query` over the
client's bodies **at the predicted tick's pose**, which the client computes from the last
`Body_Motion` (the same deterministic motion code as the server: a path is exact; a constant
velocity is exact until the server changes it). Riding carries the predicted player by the
same rider rule. When the server changes a body's motion (a script's new command), the
client learns it a round trip later and reconciles as for any other correction.

**Scripts** (capability `structure`, server side, API version recorded by M52e):
`structure.capture(box, pivot) -> body`, `structure.release(body)`,
`structure.move(body, {velocity, yaw_rate})`, `structure.move_to(body, pos, yaw, ticks)`,
`structure.stop(body)`, `structure.info(body)` (pose, size, riders, blocked),
`structure.set_block(body, local_x, local_y, local_z, block)` (charged like `world.edit`);
events `structure_blocked {body}`, `structure_arrived {body}`, `structure_rider {body,
player, on}`. Parameters: `region` (capture boxes and every pose stay inside it), `alive`
(bodies at once), `blocks` (per body). The script's own bodies only (`Origin`), like
`entity.control`. A client script steers a vehicle by sending its input on a channel (M52d)
that the server script turns into `structure.move`.

**Saves and the journal.** Bodies are saved with their chunk's entities (the region chunk
record of the chunk holding the pivot), their grids and block entities inside, in the save
transaction (M46's crash points extended); a world unloading with a moving body stops it
first. Capture, release and `structure.set_block` are journal actions (M50c's
`Edit_System.Body`); a body's motion is not journaled (it is state, kept in the save).
Rollback by cause releases a body the cause captured, at its captured pose, and restores the
world hash; M52b's `/rollback script <id>` covers bodies.

**Order of the steps:**

1. `Body_Shape` and `Body_Motion`, interest, the dissector, the bandwidth scene.
2. The body mesh and its drawing with yaw; the visual baseline scene `body` (a door at 0
   and 45 degrees, a lift between floors).
3. The client's `Body_Query` and prediction against bodies; riding.
4. The `structure` capability, bindings and events; hostile rows.
5. Saves, unload, rollback.
6. Benchmark.

**Tests:**

- `test_ride_a_lift_with_latency` (two clients over UDP, 10% loss, 100 ms): both ride a lift
  up and down 20 blocks; the riding client's prediction corrections stay under 1/64 block
  (counted by `Prediction_Check`); both see the lift where the server has it within the
  interpolation delay.
- `test_door_swings_for_both` (stepped): a scripted door swings; the second client sees the
  same pose at each snapshot; a player pushed by it on the server is pushed the same in the
  client's prediction.
- Save and load a world with a moving lift and a rider: the lift resumes its path at the same
  tick; `test_save_survives_crashes` with bodies; rollback by cause restores the hash.
- Script tests: every binding and event; hostile rows (in M52's suite): capture outside the
  region, capture of another pack's protected area, 1 000 captures a tick (denied past
  `alive`), `move_to` with NaN, moving another pack's body, `set_block` of an unbreakable
  block.
- The determinism scenario with bodies and scripts.

**Benchmarks.** Client: a scenario `bodies` in `client/benchmark.odin` (16 bodies of 4 096
blocks moving in view); limit: the bodies' share of `frame_record` under 0.2 ms and their
GPU cost reported (`gpu_held`). Server: M50c's `budget_bodies` gains replication to 16
players; limit: bytes a tick per player under the bandwidth baseline's session plus the
records' size, listed.

**Open questions for the owner:** none beyond M50c's. M50c's question 4 (bodies pushing each
other) does not change this row.

**Left out:** an unreliable channel for driving (measured here: if `test_ride_a_lift` and a
driving test show a reliable round trip makes a vehicle feel late, an unreliable channel is
proposed as a row, not added); sound of moving structures (audio rows); bodies on the GPU
(R15).

**Exit check:** the tests above pass; `build bench budgets -check` and the `bodies` client
scenario within their limits; the `body` visual baseline accepted and catching a change; the
suite, `build check`, `build determinism`; CI green.

**Check by hand:** in a world with `server/testdata/packs/lift_demo` installed and granted,
stand on the lift and use its button: it rises smoothly with you on it, with no jitter under
your feet; walk into the swinging door: it pushes you, never through you.
