# M50f: entity framework v2, part three (entities belong to chunks: handoff, unload and load, saved in the regions)

**Why.** Split from M50 (2026-10-30, #85). After M50e every entity lives in the archetype
store, but all of them stay in memory forever (a sheep far from everyone waits, frozen) and
the save is still one `entities.cfg` per world. This row makes a chunk own its entities: they
leave memory with it and come back with it, saved in M46's region chunk records. Memory is
then bounded by the loaded area, not by everything ever spawned.

**What this builds on:** M50e (`Entity_World`, the store, the spatial service with
`spatial_chunk`, the `freeze` system, `Kind_Id`, `Component_Info.release`), M50
(`store_spawn_with_id`, `store_committed_bytes`), M46's region chunk records
(`write_chunk_payload`/`read_chunk_payload` in `common/game/block_entity.odin`: blocks, then
block entities; M49 adds `layers` and `updates`), the save transaction (`server/save_tx.odin`,
`server/save_slice.odin`), the ledger's scopes (`.Scope_Out`, `.Scope_In`, `.Void`),
`unload_unwanted`, `unload_left` and `regen`, `OLDER_BUILD` (`server/migrate.odin`), and
today's `entities.cfg` (`server/save.odin`: `ENTITIES_FILE`, `write_entities`,
`load_entities`). The session first checks M50e as built and corrects this spec in the same
commit.

## Design (M50f)

- **Ownership.** Every entity belongs to the chunk its `Transform` position is in (the
  spatial service's bucket: one source).
- **Handoff** (the `freeze` system becomes `handoff`; each tick, for entities whose chunk
  changed this tick and for every entity of a chunk whose readiness changed): `Frozen` is
  added when the chunk or the one below is not ready and removed when both are (today's
  `chunk_ready`).
- **A chunk that unloads takes its entities with it.** On unload (`unload_unwanted`,
  `unload_left`, `regen`), the chunk's entities of saved kinds are encoded (below) into
  `w.saved_entities[pos]` (heap bytes, as `w.saved` holds edited blocks), their stacks leave
  the ledger's scope (`.Scope_Out`), and they are despawned without a death (no event, no
  drop, `release` run for each component); entities of kinds not saved (a rocket in flight)
  are dropped, logged once per tick with the count. When the chunk is ready again, its saved
  entities come back in saved order with their ids (`store_spawn_with_id`) and `.Scope_In`.
  A player entity is never unloaded (its chunk is in its own interest area).
- **Encoding** (`common/game/entity_save.odin`, used by unloading and the region record
  alike): `varint n`, then per entity `varint id`, `varint kind` (an index into the record's
  palette of kind ids, M47's rule for names in saves), `varint c`, then c components, each
  `varint component` (an index into a palette of component names), `varint length` and the
  value in the M39 codec's create encoding (every field absolute). Saved components:
  `Transform`, `Physics`, `Health`, `Age`, `Wool`, `Genes`, `Breeding`, `Stack`, `Natural`,
  `Fields`, `Owner`, `Origin`, `Tags` (as names, through the record's palette of strings) (not `Brain`, `Frozen`, `Hurt_By` or `Player_Link`); a
  component's `Component_Info` gains `saved: bool` and an `encode`/`decode` pair for one
  that holds a handle (M50d's bodies). Decoding refuses (the record's entities dropped with
  one warning naming the region and the reason, blocks kept) a count past `MAX_ENTITIES`, a
  length past the rest of the record, a palette index past its palette, a component of the
  wrong size, a value its kind's `check` refuses (a stack of an unknown item: `.Void` in the
  ledger).
- **The save:** M46's region chunk record gains an `entities` section after M49's sections.
  A chunk record may now hold entities and no blocks (an unedited chunk: its blocks
  regenerate). Loaded chunks' entities are encoded when the save captures the chunk (the save
  transaction's capture, so a save is consistent with the tick it was taken at). On load a
  kind the world no longer has is dropped with one warning per kind and region (a stack in it
  through the ledger's `.Void`); an unknown component is skipped by its length.
  `Entity_Store.next_id` is saved with the world (M46's world record).
- **No migration** (before 1.0 nothing is compatible): `entities.cfg` is no longer read or
  written, and its code goes. A world directory that still has one is refused with
  `OLDER_BUILD`; the region format version is bumped, and a region of the older version is
  refused the same way.

## The order of the steps (M50f)

1. `common/game/entity_save.odin` (encode, decode, refusals) and its unit tests and fuzz
   target.
2. Handoff; unload and load with entities; the ledger scopes.
3. The region section, `next_id` in the world record, `entities.cfg` removed, the refusals.
4. The memory test and the benchmark scene.

This row is already a part: it is not split again.

## Left out (M50f)

Entities travelling between worlds; simulating entities in unloaded chunks (they wait, as
the world waits); bodies' grids in the record (M50d adds them through `encode`/`decode`);
quantized motion (M50b).

## Tests (M50f)

- `common/game/entity_save_test.odin`: every saved component round-trips; palettes;
  unknown components skipped by length; each refusal above refuses (a table), never crashes.
- Fuzz (Q1's harness): target `save.entities` (`fuzz_entity_records` in
  `common/game/fuzz.odin`, listed in `tools/fuzz/targets.odin`), the bytes as an `entities`
  section against the base registry; generator: a section of 64 entities of every kind
  written by the encoder. Contract (Q1's) plus: a decoded entity's every component passes its
  kind's check. `test_fuzz_save_entities_smoke` in the suite; the regression files on three
  OSes. `save.region`'s generator gains entities.
- `server/entity_persist_test.odin` (stepped harness):
  - `test_entities_unload_with_chunks`: 20 sheep and 5 stacks in chunks the player walks
    away from leave memory with them (`store_count` drops by 25; `Frozen` never set on an
    entity whose chunks are ready), come back with their ids and every saved component's
    value when the player returns, and the ledger audit balances at every tick.
  - `test_entities_saved_in_regions`: entities in unedited chunks saved (the chunk record has
    no blocks) and loaded after a restart on the directory; an unknown kind dropped with its
    one warning and its stack voided in the ledger; `next_id` continues after the restart
    (no id reused).
  - `test_entities_save_consistent`: a save started at tick T while sheep walk across a
    chunk border during its slices holds each sheep exactly once, at its tick-T position.
  - `test_old_entities_file_refused`: a world directory with `entities.cfg` is refused with
    `OLDER_BUILD`.
  - `test_save_survives_crashes` (M46's) with entities: every crash point leaves the world
    loading with either the old or the new entities, never a mix.
- `server/entity_load_test.odin`, `test_memory_flat_over_churn`: a stepped server; 20 rounds
  of summoning 2 000 sheep on a stone platform, 100 ticks, `/kill voxiverse:sheep`, 20 ticks,
  and every fifth round the player walks 200 blocks away and back (their chunks unload and
  load). The store's, the spatial service's and the world arena's committed bytes after
  round 2 equal those after round 20 exactly, as do the mirror's.
- Unchanged in their assertions and passing: the M50e list, `server/save_test.odin`,
  `save_tx_test.odin`, `export_test.odin` (an export carries the regions, so the entities).

## Benchmarks (M50f)

`budget_entity_chunks` in `server/budgets.odin`, judged by `build bench budgets -check`: 64
chunks holding 2 000 sheep and 500 stacks, unloaded and loaded again 20 times.

| Metric | What | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| `Entity_Unload` | CPU us to unload one chunk's entities (encode, scope out, despawn) | 400 |
| `Entity_Load` | CPU us to bring one chunk's entities back | 400 |

The existing `Save_Capture` budget (its scene's 400 entities are now encoded into regions)
must still hold; `budget_save_scene`'s entities stay at 400.

## Exit check (M50f)

- `test_entities_unload_with_chunks`, `test_entities_saved_in_regions`,
  `test_entities_save_consistent` and `test_memory_flat_over_churn` pass; the ledger audit
  balances in all.
- The `save.entities` fuzz smoke target is clean.
- `build bench budgets -check` within the two limits above and the save's.
- `build determinism`, `build check`, the suite and CI green.

## Check by hand (M50f)

In a world: walk far from a flock of sheep and back: the same sheep (same colours, same
lambs) are where they were; quit and reopen the world: they are still there; a stack dropped
and left lies where it was.
