## Design (M53d)

### Spawners (`server/spawners/*.json`)

```json
{
  "den": {
    "kind": "voxiverse:sheep",
    "region": "demo:den",
    "tag": "den",
    "max_alive": 3,
    "every": 100,
    "total": 0,
    "active": "manual",
    "min_distance": 6,
    "on_empty": [{"say": "The den is quiet."}]
  }
}
```

- `active`: `always`, `manual` (started and stopped by `spawner_start`/`spawner_stop`), or
  `{"near": <blocks>}` (while a player is within that many blocks of the region). `total` 0 is
  unlimited. At most 64 spawners a world, `max_alive` at most 64.
- Each attempt picks a column in the region with the gameplay RNG, takes the ground there
  with `game.landing_spot`, requires the chunk loaded and `min_distance` from every player; 8
  tries, then the next `every`. Spawns through the data runner's `entity.spawn` (its kinds,
  alive and per-second budgets) with the tag; the spawner keeps the `Entity_Id`s. `on_empty`
  runs and `spawner_empty` fires when its last entity dies after at least one spawned.
- State in `Gameplay_State` (active, spawned total, next tick, ids); the entities persist with
  M50f's; ids that no longer resolve on load are dropped.
- Actions `spawner_start`, `spawner_stop <spawner>` (`entity.spawn`); trigger `spawner_empty`
  (`spawner`; no player in context).

### Encounters (`server/encounters/*.json`)

```json
{
  "ambush": {
    "region": "demo:den",
    "start": "manual",
    "waves": [
      {"spawn": ["voxiverse:sheep 3 tag ambush"]},
      {"delay": 60, "spawn": ["voxiverse:sheep 5 tag ambush"]}
    ],
    "timeout": 3600,
    "absent": 600,
    "on_cleared": ["loot demo:reward"],
    "on_failed": [{"message": "They got away."}],
    "reset_after": 6000
  }
}
```

- Started by `encounter_start` or `"start": "enter"` (a player entering the region);
  participants are the players in the region at the start. A wave starts when the previous one
  is all dead (after its `delay`); the last wave dead is cleared (`on_cleared` for each
  participant, the `encounter_cleared` trigger); past `timeout`, or with no participant in the
  region for `absent` ticks, it fails (its entities removed, `on_failed`, `encounter_failed`).
- States `Idle, Running(wave, ids, started), Cleared, Failed`, back to `Idle` after
  `reset_after`; saved in `Gameplay_State`. At most 32 encounters a world, 8 waves each, 64
  entities a wave.
- Actions `encounter_start`, `encounter_stop <encounter>` (`entity.spawn`; `encounter_stop`
  also `entity.control`).

### The demo pack (`server/testdata/packs/demo/`)

Files on disk (read with `pack_read_dir`; M52b's hand check already names this directory):
`pack.json` (`"api"` as M53 decided, `"title": "Demo Quests"`) declaring `world.read`,
`world.edit` (a box around the village), `entity.read`, `entity.spawn` (`voxiverse:sheep`,
`demo:elder`), `entity.control`, `player.read`, `player.teleport`, `item.give`
(`voxiverse:wheat`, `voxiverse:seeds`, `voxiverse:feather_charm`), `item.take`, `chat.send`,
`ui.notice`, `ui.prompt`, `store`, `rules.set` (`names` empty: its own rule only),
`command.register`; an entity kind `demo:elder` (`data/entities/elder.json`: a box model, no
brain, invulnerable); spawner `elder_post` (`demo:elder`, one, `always`, a one-block region);
regions `well`, `den`, `village`; M53b's quest `village` (with `spawner_start demo:den`) and
dialogue `elder`; loot tables `reward` (wheat, seeds, a 1-weight feather charm) and `ruin`
(`per_hour` 4); triggers `elder_talk` (`"on": "entity_used", "kind": "demo:elder", "do":
["dialogue demo:elder"]`) and `bonus` (`"on": "command", "name": "bonus", "do": [{"loot":
"demo:ruin", "else": [{"message": "The ruin is empty for now."}]}]`); death cause `zap` with
its own lines and a `player_died` trigger on it (gives a marker item); rule `wolves`. It
passes `./build.sh packs` (`tools/packcheck`) and is what M54b's lighthouse is measured
against.

### The two-player UDP scenario

`server/gameplay_net_test.odin`, `test_quest_chain_two_players`, on M39's `Net_Client`
helpers: a listening stepped server, cheats for alice only, the demo pack enabled in the
world and granted `all`; `Net_Client` learns the `Prompt` and `Quests` records and sends
`Answer`. Each step waits with `net_wait` for at most 300 ticks.

1. Both join; the elder is there within 30 ticks (an entity record of kind `demo:elder`).
2. alice uses the elder: a `Prompt` arrives with the four choices filtered to "I will." and
   "Goodbye."; she answers 0; her `Quests` record shows "Find the old well"; bob's shows
   nothing (quests are per character).
3. alice teleports into `demo:well` (`/tp`): the stage becomes "Drive off the sheep of the
   den 0/3"; the den spawns three sheep tagged `den`.
4. bob kills two den sheep (`Attack` until their death record): alice's progress stays 0/3.
   alice kills three (the spawner refills to three): 3/3, the stage becomes "Return to the
   elder".
5. alice uses the elder, answers "The den is quiet now.", then "Farewell.": the quest is
   completed, her inventory holds the reward's rolls (the exact items for the test's seed),
   the world chat shows "[Demo Quests] alice helped the elder." to both.
6. bob runs `/bonus` five times: four loot rolls arrive, the fifth gives the notice "The
   ruin is empty for now."
7. alice leaves and joins again: her quest reads as completed (the elder offers only
   "Goodbye."); bob is unaffected.
8. alice runs `/damage alice 100 demo:zap`: the chat shows the cause's own line, her death
   screen shows it, and after Respawn the marker item is in her inventory; bob's chat has that one
   line and nothing else. `/damage alice 100 voxiverse:fall` then gives today's fall line and
   no marker.
9. With a prompt open, alice dies: her `Prompt` record closes; she presses Respawn
   (`Client_Kind.Respawn`) and is at the spawn with full health within 30 ticks.
10. The ledger audit balances, with `Loot`, `Script_Give` and `Kit` entries equal to what the
    clients' inventories gained; the desync counter is 0.

A second form, `test_quest_chain_two_players_loss`, runs the same steps with 10% loss both
ways (M39's lossy pipe): the same end state.

8 encounters running; the scene fails unless every spawner reached `max_alive` once. New

## Check by hand (M53d)

In singleplayer, install the demo pack in a new world (copy `server/testdata/packs/demo` into
the universe's `packs/`, list it in `universe.cfg`'s `packs`, then `/pack enable demo` and
`/pack grant demo all`): talk to the elder with the mouse and then with a pad, do the chain,
run `/bonus` five times, die with `/damage <your name> 100 demo:zap` and press Respawn. The
dialogue panel, the tracker and the death screen with the pack's line read well at UI scales
1 and 2.
