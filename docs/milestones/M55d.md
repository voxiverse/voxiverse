# M55d: validation, rate limits, melee lag compensation and the hostile client

The second of two rows ([M55](M55.md), M55d; split from M55 by #88 at the joint the old M55
spec named: its steps 4-7). After M55, before M55b.

**Why.** The server's defences against a hostile client are scattered: decode checks,
`game.INPUT_BUDGET_MAX`, `MAX_QUEUED_ACTIONS` (`server/stream.odin`), reach in `apply_edit`
(`server/actions.odin`) and `entity_reachable` (`server/entities.odin`), `FIRE_EVERY`
(`server/actions.odin`), M13's chat tokens, M27's report windows, M31's inventory fuzz, 17
`disconnect(s, c, ...)` calls with their own texts, and M52d's per-channel script-message
buckets with a flood disconnect of their own. Nothing is logged in one place, nothing adds
up to a kick, and no per-kind rate limit exists. This row puts them under one framework, adds
limits per message kind, compensates melee for latency as M48h already compensates shots,
and proves it with a hostile bot.

**What this builds on:** M55's transport (a forged or replayed packet never reaches a
handler; `Conn_Stats`' `auth_failed`, `replayed`), M54b's `bot/` (`Bot`, verbs, `Behaviour`,
harness parties on `Simnet`), M52's `Bucket` (integers in thousandths, refilled from a tick
count, no clock), M52d's channels (`Script_Send`, per player and channel buckets, the
`too many script messages` disconnect, `Script_Message_Flood`), M48h's lag compensation
(`server/rewind.odin`: `Rewind`, `rewind_record`, `rewind_ticks(latency_ms)`,
`rewind_frame`, `REWIND_MAX_TICKS :: 8`; `server/weapons.odin`'s `shooter_latency_ms` and
`shot_trace`, players and creatures both targets), M38's desync detector, the harness and
`netcode.Conditions`.

## Checked against the code (2026-10-31, #88; corrections to the old M55 text)

- **Lag compensation exists** for hitscan (`Shoot`) since M48h: a ring of the last ticks'
  player and creature positions, rewound by the server's own estimate of the shooter's
  latency, capped at 8 ticks. The old text's `view_tick`/`view_alpha` fields on `Attack` and
  a second ring are **not built**. Decided (engineering, consistent with M48h): melee
  (`Attack`) and `Use_Entity` reach checks use the same ring and the same server-side
  estimate, so a client cannot claim a rewind and nothing is added to the protocol.
- **Players are targets** (M48h's weapons hit players); the old "no attack on players"
  is stale. `Attack` still targets entities only.
- `Client_Message` today: `Hello`, `Input`, `Ping`, `Teleport`, `Set_Rule`, `Edit`,
  `Command`, `Attack`, `Fire`, `Chat`, `Inventory_Request`, `Proof`, `Choose`, `Dial`,
  `Use_Entity`, `Stats_Request`, `Telemetry_Report`, `Telemetry_Batch`, `Use_Block`,
  `Prediction_Check`, `Pack_Want`, `Packs_Ready`, `Respawn`, `Shoot`, `Reload`; M52d adds
  `Script_Send`, M53b its dialogue choice. The table below covers all of them.
- An `Input.snapshot` past the server's tick is already ignored (`handle`'s `Input` case);
  it becomes a violation instead of a silent skip.
- **M52d's buckets and flood disconnect are absorbed** (#88): `Script_Send` is one kind in
  this row's table (the sum over channels, M52d's engine ceiling); M52d's per-(pack,
  channel) buckets stay as the kind's sub-buckets, in the same `Limits` structure; M52d's
  "ten times the rate for five seconds" disconnect becomes this row's general
  `Flood` violation for every kind; `Script_Message_Flood` stays as the telemetry kind
  M52d appended, now raised by the framework.
- **No timing in tests (T5):** the old hostile test's "tick p99 under `TICK_DURATION`" moves
  to the `hostile` budget scene.

## Design

### The validation framework (`server/validate.odin`)

- `Check :: enum u8 {Decode, Order, Rate, Bytes, Movement, Reach, Aim, Edit, Inventory,
  Action, Chat, Script, Desync}`.
- `Violation :: enum u16`, append-only. `VIOLATION_INFO: [Violation]Violation_Info {name,
  check, weight: f32, text}` (`text` is what the kick tells the player).
- `violation(s, c, v, kind := Client_Kind(0), value: f64 = 0, limit: f64 = 0)` is the one
  call every check makes. It adds the weight to `c.score`, writes the reasons log, and kicks
  at the threshold. Every one of today's `disconnect(s, c, ...)` for a protocol breach goes
  through it (a test greps that `handle` has no other `disconnect` for one); refusals that
  are not breaches (a full server, a name taken, a wrong password) stay plain disconnects.
- `c.score` decays by half every 60 s of ticks (per tick, × 0.5^(1/1800), from the tick
  count: no clock). At 100 or more the client is disconnected with "refused: <text of the
  violation that crossed it>", and the line's `action` is `kicked`.
- The violations and their weights:
  - 100 (an instant kick): `Decode_Failed`, `Before_Hello`, `Unexpected_Handshake`,
    `Batch_To_Server` (a client's `Telemetry_Batch`), `Flood` (a kind's messages over ten
    times its rate for five seconds in a row: M52d's rule, for every kind);
  - 2 per second with drops (not per message): `Rate_Exceeded` (with the kind);
  - 2: `Fire_Too_Fast`, `Aim_Off` (logged, never refuses);
  - 20: `Peer_Flooding` (from netcode, below), `Snapshot_Ack_Future`, `Look_Invalid` (yaw or
    pitch NaN or out of range: decode catches some);
  - 1 a tick: `Input_Overrun` (the queue full, or inputs arriving faster than 1.2 a tick over
    10 s: a sped-up client);
  - 1: `Input_Out_Of_Order`;
  - 10: `Fly_Not_Allowed`, `Action_Queue_Full`;
  - 5: `Action_Ahead` (an action whose `after` is more than 60 ahead of the newest input
    received), `Reach_Edit`, `Reach_Entity`, `Reach_Block_Use`, `Inventory_Invalid` (a slot
    or action that cannot exist), `Script_Message_Invalid` (M52d's step 3 refusals: wrong
    type, range, handle);
  - 3: `Chat_Flood` (M13's limiter refused a line);
  - 10 each beyond 3 a minute: `Desync_Repeated` (M38's detector).
- Not violations (weight 0, debug log only): an edit refused by the rules or because the
  chunk was unloaded, a late click on an entity that is gone, a script message for a halted
  script (M52d row 7). Honest players do these under latency.
- The reasons log is `<universe>/violations/YYYY-MM-DD.jsonl`, one line each: `{"t": <unix
  ms>, "tick": n, "account": "<hex>", "character": "...", "address": "...", "violation":
  "reach_edit", "check": "reach", "kind": "edit", "value": 7.9, "limit": 6.5, "weight": 5,
  "score": 23.5, "action": "dropped" | "logged" | "kicked"}`. Appended through M46's writer
  off the tick (the save writer's thread), never an `fsync` in the tick.
  - Addresses are kept because this is moderation data, not telemetry; `docs/telemetry.md`'s
    no-address rule is about the lake.
  - At most 30 lines per client per minute; the rest are counted and written as one line
    `{"violation": "suppressed", "count": n}`.
  - A server with no directory keeps the last 256 in memory, for the tests and the command.
  - `/violations [player] [n]` prints recent lines (`server.violations`).

### Rate limits per message kind (`server/limits.odin`)

- `Limits` per playing client: one M52 `Bucket` per kind, counted in ticks with integer
  milli-tokens; `Script_Send` holds M52d's sub-buckets per (pack, channel) beside its own.
- Over the limit, the message is dropped. An `Edit` gets the `Edit_Result` rejection, so the
  client rolls back; a `Script_Send` counts M52d's `throttled`. `Rate_Exceeded` is raised at
  most once a second per kind; `Flood` when a kind's drops exceed ten times its rate for five
  seconds in a row.
- The table is constants, sized from what the game's own client sends (each row's note):

| Kind | Per second | Burst | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Input | 36 | 60 | 30 are due; the input budget still applies |
| Ping | 4 | 8 | |
| Teleport | 10 | 20 | permissioned too |
| Set_Rule | 5 | 20 | |
| Edit | 20 | 40 | creative breaks at about 5/s held |
| Command | 5 | 10 | |
| Attack | 20 | 20 | |
| Fire | 10 | 10 | `FIRE_EVERY` stays |
| Shoot | 36 | 60 | sent per tick while held; the weapon's `fire_rate` stays the server's |
| Reload, Respawn | 2 | 4 | each |
| Chat | 5 | 10 | M13's own tokens stay, with their message |
| Inventory_Request | 40 | 80 | drag-spreading sends bursts |
| Dial | 10 | 20 | |
| Use_Entity, Use_Block | 10 | 20 | each |
| Stats_Request | 2 | 4 | |
| Telemetry_Report | 1 | 4 | M27's window stays |
| Prediction_Check | 2 | 4 | one a second is sent |
| Pack_Want | 40 | 80 | a join's pack parts; only while fetching |
| Script_Send | 20 | 40 | M52d's engine ceiling, all channels; each channel's own rate within it |
| M53b's dialogue choice | 5 | 10 | |
| Hello, Proof, Choose, Packs_Ready | once | | a second is `Unexpected_Handshake` |

- Bytes from a peer, in netcode (`host.odin`): 128 KiB/s, burst 256 KiB, counted before
  opening. Over that, packets are dropped and counted (`Conn_Stats.over_rate`); sustained
  over it for 2 s, the host raises `Peer_Flooding {peer}` as a new `Host_Event`, which the
  server turns into the violation.
- A test asserts the table covers every `Client_Message` variant (a `#partial`-free switch
  over the union's kinds): a message added later without a row fails it.

### Melee lag compensation (on M48h's ring)

- `entity_reachable(c, ent)` gains the rewound box: the entity's position in
  `rewind_frame(s, w, rewind_ticks(shooter_latency_ms(s, c)))` (the same estimate shots use).
  The hit or use is accepted if the player's eye is within reach plus `EDIT_REACH_SLACK` of
  the rewound box or the current one; otherwise `Reach_Entity`.
- Aim is checked, not enforced, for `Attack`: the ray from the eye along the look of the input
  the attack follows must pass within 0.3 blocks of the rewound or current box, or `Aim_Off`
  is logged.
- Rockets (`Fire`) are not compensated: the server simulates them from the input tick's
  position already. Shots keep M48h's code unchanged.
- `rewind_record` is in the world loop; M55b keeps it there (phase 2).

### The hostile client (`bot/hostile.odin`, a behaviour)

One step every 30 ticks after joining, each step counted. Over the harness's `Simnet` (and
over UDP in one test) it sends:

- a truncated message; an unknown message kind;
- 100 inputs in one tick; an input with a NaN yaw; an input asking to fly without permission;
- 200 edits in a second; an edit 20 blocks away;
- an attack on an entity 30 blocks away;
- an inventory request on slot 9999;
- 60 chat lines in a second;
- an action 500 inputs ahead; an `Input.snapshot` from the future;
- a `Telemetry_Batch`;
- 1 000 `Script_Send`s in one tick, and malformed ones (M52d's rows 1-3 reused, now through
  the framework);
- 120 `Shoot`s in one second;
- a second `Hello`.

`bot/hostile.odin` also has `hostile_raw(b, bytes)` for messages the encoder cannot make; the
verbs stay honest.

## Tests

- `server/validate_test.odin`: each violation, made by hand, writes its line with the right
  fields; the score halves in 1 800 ticks; 100 kicks, with the text; suppression after 30
  lines a minute; `handle` has no breach `disconnect` outside `violation`.
- `server/limits_test.odin`: per kind, the burst passes and the next message is dropped; the
  rate refills exactly over ticks; an `Edit` over the limit gets its rejection;
  `Rate_Exceeded` once a second, not per message; `Flood` after five seconds at ten times;
  the table covers every `Client_Message` kind; M52d's messaging hostile suite (rows 1-10)
  passes unchanged through the framework.
- `common/netcode/host_test.odin`: `Peer_Flooding` after 2 s over the byte limit, stepped.
- `server/latency_test.odin`, extended, `test_honest_play_clean`: the M38 script at 200 ms
  with jitter (`netcode.Conditions`), the determinism session's script, and four `script`
  bots: the score stays under 10 and no violation of weight 5 or more is written.
- `test_honest_property` (`server/validate_test.odin`): 200 seeds; a seed draws latency (0 to
  250 ms), jitter, 0 to 10% loss and a mix of honest behaviours (`script`, the party run's,
  M55e's `wander` once built) for 3 000 ticks; no honest client is ever kicked and no
  violation of weight 5 or more is written. A false positive found here is fixed in the
  check, never by a raised threshold.
- `server/lagcomp_test.odin`: a sheep moved at a steady 4 blocks/s (test-only velocity) and a
  client at 150 ms each way, interpolating. It attacks when its drawn sheep is 0.2 inside
  reach and the server's current sheep is outside it: accepted. With the client's latency
  estimate forced to 0: refused with `Reach_Entity`. At 600 ms: the rewind is capped at
  `REWIND_MAX_TICKS` and the far hit refused.
- `server/hostile_test.odin` (on the harness, and one run over UDP):
  - every step of the hostile script is refused or dropped and logged with its expected
    violation (a table: step → violation, action);
  - the hostile client is kicked by the step whose weight crosses 100, before the script
    ends, with that violation's text;
  - an honest bot connected meanwhile sees no correction and no disconnect, and the world's
    state hash equals a run without the hostile bot (its refused messages changed nothing);
  - no leak (tracking allocator).

## Fuzzing (Q1)

`net.client_messages_validated` in `build fuzz` (beside Q2's protocol targets, which it
extends with the server's handlers): random and mutated sequences of `Client_Message`s from a
`Playing` client of a harness: never a crash or a leak; the client is either still playing
with a score below 100 or kicked with a violation's text; no other client's state hash
changes. A line in `docs/fuzzing.md`.

## Budgets (`build bench budgets`; T5)

`budget_hostile` (`server/budgets.odin`): 16 honest `script` bots and 4 hostile bots
restarting their scripts on one harness for 3 000 ticks, CPU time of the stepping thread.
Metrics: `Hostile_Tick` (the server's slowest tick, us) limit **33 000** (a tick: hostile
clients cannot make the server fall behind); `Validate_Share` (the framework's and limits'
share of the server's tick CPU, %) limit **2**. The scene's test calls it for what it does
(every hostile bot kicked, every honest one playing).

## Left out

Bans that follow from violations (kicks only; bans stay the moderators'); anti-cheat on the
client; claimed view ticks for melee (the server's estimate decides, as for shots); per-IP
limits across accounts (M55's handshake limit and the byte limit cover a flooder's traffic).
