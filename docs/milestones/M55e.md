# M55e: bandwidth budgets, raised limits and the 200-bot load run (with scripts and messages)

The second of two rows ([M55b](M55b.md), M55e; split from M55b by #88 at the joint the old
M55b spec had: its steps 3-6). After M55b, before Q7 (the soak builds on this row's bot
process and `build load`).

**Why.** Sends are bounded only by `SEND_BUDGET_BYTES` (64 KiB, `server/stream.odin`) for
chunks, `game.SNAPSHOT_PARTS` (8) for motion and `Conn.rate`; there is no per-client budget
across them and no fair share of the server's upstream. `game.MAX_PLAYERS` is 32. The only
load test is `dedicated/bench.odin`'s `BENCH_BOTS` (8) bots for the `server` and `party`
scenarios. A server of 200 players running packs' scripts and their messages has never run.

**What this builds on:** M55b's phases and world threads (outboxes per client, drained in
phase 3), M54b's `bot/` (UDP links: the benchmark's bots already moved onto it), M55's
transport (`Net_Crypto_Us`), M55d's limits (the load bots stay under every one), M52's
sandbox and M52d's channels, M39's replication (`common/game/replica.odin`:
`sender_flush`, nearest-first and waiting priority), `server/stream.odin` (`stream`,
`MAX_BACKLOG`), `common/netcode/conn.odin` (`Conn.rate`), `dedicated/main.odin`'s `Options`
(`max_players` says "at most 32").

## Design

### Bandwidth budgets and priorities (`server/send.odin`)

- Each tick each client gets a byte budget `client_rate / TICK_RATE`, where `client_rate =
  min(net.client_rate, fair share)`.
- `net.client_rate` (default 512 KiB/s) and `net.server_rate` (default 48 MiB/s) are config
  variables, `--client-rate` and `--server-rate` on the dedicated server.
- The fair share is `net.server_rate` divided by the playing clients. What a client left
  unused last tick is shared among the others the next tick, at most double a share.
- The order within a client's budget (drained in M55b's phase 3 from the client's outbox):
  1. Control and answers: Welcome, Reject, Edit_Result, Pong, Characters, Challenge. Never
     deferred; they count.
  2. The reliable lane (M39), including M52d's `Script_Deliver`. Never deferred: its deltas
     build on each other. It counts.
  3. The snapshot lane. `sender_flush` gains `snapshot_limit` bytes: the remaining budget,
     clamped to between one part and `SNAPSHOT_PARTS` parts. Records that do not fit wait,
     and M39's nearest-first and waiting priority picks them next tick.
  4. Chunks. `stream()`'s budget is `min(SEND_BUDGET_BYTES, remaining)`. `MAX_BACKLOG` stays.
  5. Bulk (`Stats_Text`, M27 answers, pack parts) only if bytes remain, else next tick.
- `Conn.rate` is set to 1.25 × `client_rate`, so resends fit and the connection never drops
  what the scheduler chose.
- Counted per client: bytes sent per lane per second. Metrics `Server_Sent_Client_KiB_P99`
  and `Server_Budget_Overruns` (ticks where control and reliable alone exceeded the budget,
  counted, never refused).

### Limits raised

- `game.MAX_PLAYERS :: 256` (the protocol's player ids already fit); `--max-players` help
  says "at most 256". `Config.max_players` keeps the default of 8 (`DEFAULT_MAX_PLAYERS`).
- Chunk capacity per world becomes `min(per_player × max_players + workers ×
  MAX_IN_FLIGHT_PER_WORKER, MAX_WORLD_CHUNKS)`, with `MAX_WORLD_CHUNKS :: 131072`. A chunk
  that does not fit waits in the stream, nearest first, logged once a minute as "world
  full".
- `players_put` (`server/players.odin`) walks `w.players`, not every client.

### The load pack (`testdata/packs/load/`, signed in the test with a key made there)

Built only from what packs and scripts can do, so the load run measures them: enabled in
every world of the load universe; a server script that every 2 s sets and clears a block in
a 16 × 16 region near spawn, keeps a per-player count of the blocks each bot breaks (the
`block_broken` event), gives a bot entering the region one marker item through its declared
faucet (a cap of 1 per character); a channel `ping` (`to: server`, 2 a second) the bots send
on once a second, answered by a `score` channel (`to: client`) broadcast once a second with
the world's top counts. Grants: `world.edit` in its region, `players.read`, `items.give`
(capped), `channel`.

### The load harness

- Bots in a process of their own: `voxiverse-server --bots <n> --connect <host:port>
  [--seconds s] [--threads k] [--report <file>]` (`dedicated/load.odin`): `n` M54b bots on
  UDP links, split over `k` threads (default 4), each pumping its bots. Bots keep no chunks
  and do not predict, so a bot costs its decoding only.
- Each bot is a `wander` bot (`bot/wander.odin`): it walks in a direction from its RNG,
  changing every 5 to 15 s, within 160 blocks of its world's spawn (it turns back past
  that); it jumps at random and looks around; every 20 s it places stone ahead or breaks
  the block it placed; every 60 s it says a chat line; once a second it sends `ping` on the
  load pack's channel (M52d's `Script_Send` through M54b's `bot_script_send`) and checks the
  `score` it receives decodes; every 120 s it travels (`/travel <address>`) to another of the
  load universe's four worlds, entering the pack's region on the way half the time.
- Bots join 10 a second.
- `build load [--bots 200] [--minutes 10]` (`tools/build/load.odin`):
  1. Makes a fresh `build/load/universe` with four worlds (the home and three addresses
     fixed in the tool), the load pack installed and enabled in each, and a
     `permissions.cfg` giving the role `bot` `travel`, `chat.send` and `world.edit`.
  2. Starts `voxiverse-server --universe build/load/universe --max-players 256 --view-radius
     6 --view-height 3 --stats-out build/load/server.jsonl`.
  3. Starts the bot process, and stops both at the end (killed and reaped on any failure).
- `--stats-out` is a new server flag: a JSON line every 10 s, with tick p50, p99 and max for
  the last 10 s, each world's tick p99, phase 1 and 3 times, clients, bytes sent per client
  (p50, p99, max), budget overruns, crypto us per tick (M55), the arena's used and reserved
  bytes, the "behind" count; and per world the scripts' instructions per tick (p99) and
  suspensions, script messages received, delivered, throttled and refused (M52d's counters),
  and M55d's violations.
- The bot process's report holds per bot: joined, disconnects with reasons, bytes in and out,
  edits refused, `ping`s sent and `score`s received.
- The tool writes `build/load/result.json`, `{"verdict", "thresholds", "measured", "minutes",
  "bots"}`, and exits 1 when a threshold fails.
- **The thresholds**, over the measured span (after the last bot joined, plus 60 s of
  warm-up), are budgets, not machine numbers:
  - tick p99 ≤ 33.3 ms in every 10 s window, and the mean ≤ 16 ms;
  - zero "server behind" lines; every world's tick p99 ≤ 33.3 ms;
  - mean server send ≤ 40 KiB/s per bot, no client over its budget by more than 5% in any
    10 s window, total ≤ `net.server_rate`;
  - zero bot disconnects, all 200 playing throughout; zero violations of weight 5 or more
    (M55d: the bots are honest);
  - the arena's used bytes over the last five minutes grow less than 2% (memory flat);
  - crypto ≤ 3% of tick time;
  - scripts: no suspension, no script error, instructions per tick p99 within 80% of the
    world pool; script messages: none refused, none throttled (the bots send under the
    channel's rate), every `ping` delivered, every bot received a `score` every 2 s.
- **CI:** a `load` job on ubuntu-latest (`needs: build`, timeout 25 minutes) runs `build load`
  and uploads `build/load/` as an artifact. The 4-core runner shares its cores between the
  server and the bots, which is the harder case.
- If a threshold fails there, the session profiles the run (`build trace` on the server under
  the bots) and fixes the cost it finds, the way M19 did. A threshold is not loosened without
  the owner.

## The order of the steps

1. The send scheduler, rates, `snapshot_limit`, metrics; `server/send_test.odin`.
2. Limits raised, the capacity cap.
3. `bot/wander.odin`, the load pack, `--bots`, `--stats-out`, `build load`, the CI job.
4. The 200-bot run, and whatever it finds; history.md.

## Tests

- `server/send_test.odin` (stepped, bytes counted, no clock):
  - with a 64 KiB/s client rate, the bytes sent per second of ticks stay within 5% of it
    while streaming;
  - control messages and the reliable lane are never deferred;
  - snapshot records deferred one tick arrive the next, nearest first;
  - two clients under a small `server_rate` each get half, and the unused share moves to the
    busy one;
  - property, 200 seeds: random client counts (1 to 64), rates and loads; no client exceeds
    its budget except by control and reliable bytes (counted as overruns), and every
    deferred record is sent within `SNAPSHOT_PARTS` ticks.
- `server/bandwidth_test.odin`: the baseline. With the default rates nothing in it hits a
  budget, so it is unchanged; any change is explained in the commit.
- `dedicated/load_test.odin`: the bot process's loop on the harness (its threads replaced
  by one stepped party per thread's bots, over `Simnet`): 20 `wander` bots and the load pack
  for 1 800 ticks: all play, all travel at least once, `ping`s delivered and `score`s
  received, the report written and parsed back.
- `smoke/load_test.odin` (real processes, T4b's `run_limited`): `build load --bots 8
  --minutes 1` exits 0 and writes a `result.json` that parses; a deliberately failing
  threshold (`--threshold-tick-p99 0`, a DEV flag of the tool) exits 1 naming it.
- `tools/build` test: `result.json`'s verdict from a recorded `server.jsonl` and report
  (fixtures under `tools/build/testdata/load/`): each threshold failing alone gives `fail`
  naming it.

## Budgets

`build load`'s thresholds above (CI's `load` job, which fails on any). `build bench budgets`
gains `budget_send` (`server/budgets.odin`): 64 clients' scheduling for 600 ticks, metric
`Send_Schedule` (the scheduler's CPU per client per tick, us) limit **5**.

## Left out

Priority by what a player looks at; bandwidth estimation from loss (the rate is configured,
not discovered); bots that predict at scale; more than one bot process.

## Exit check (M55e)

**Gated in CI:** `build load --bots 200 --minutes 10` passes every threshold in CI's `load`
job (and locally, its `result.json` quoted in the commit); `send_test` with its property
test; `load_test`, the smoke load test and the tool's verdict tests; the bandwidth baseline
unchanged; `build bench budgets -check` with `Send_Schedule` in budget; `build check`, the
suite and CI green on the three OSes.
