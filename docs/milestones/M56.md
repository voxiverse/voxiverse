# M56: space, part one (listener, emitters, attenuation, Doppler, the head model)

Split from the old M56: the spatial model here, the voxel acoustics (occlusion, the room's
reverb, underwater) in M56b. Both come after M48 (shapes) and M50 (entities and the spatial
query service), which they use: read their HISTORY entries for the names they built.

- **In the mixer** (`client/audio/mixer.odin`): a voice gets a spatial stage after its low-pass.
  - `Spatial :: struct {delay: [2]f32, shadow: [2]f32, notch_hz: f32, notch_db: f32}`:
    per ear a delay in frames (0..`MAX_ITD_FRAMES :: 48`) and a head-shadow coefficient,
    and the elevation notch. `set_voice_spatial(m, id, s: Spatial, at: u64 = 0)` (a new
    `Voice_Param` kind). `Play.spatial: Spatial` sets it from the start.
  - Each voice keeps a 64-frame ring of its mono signal; each ear reads it at its delay
    with linear interpolation (the delay glides across the block like gains, so a moving
    source makes no clicks; this is also where Doppler's small pitch bend from a changing
    delay comes from, which is right). Each ear then runs a one-pole, one-zero shelf with
    its `shadow` coefficient (below) and, when `notch_db != 0`, a peaking biquad. A voice
    with a zero `Spatial` skips the stage (no cost, the samples bit-identical to today:
    every baseline unchanged).
  - `capture.odin`: `CAPTURE_VERSION :: 5` (`Play`'s size changes).
  - Cost: the `audio` bench scenario gives half its voices a moving `Spatial`; it stays
    under 5% of a core.
- **The model** (`client/audio/spatial.odin`, game thread, pure functions):
  - `Listener_Pose :: struct {position, velocity, forward, up: [3]f32}` (from the camera
    each frame; `velocity` from the predicted player). `Emitter :: struct {position,
    velocity: [3]f32, class: Attenuation_Class, volume: f32}`.
  - `Spatial_Mode :: enum u8 {Speakers, Headphones}`.
  - `spatialize(l: Listener_Pose, e: Emitter, mode, rate) -> (gain: [2]f32, pitch: f32,
    cutoff: f32, s: Spatial)`.
  - Distance gain by class (`ATTENUATION :: [Attenuation_Class]Curve`, `Curve :: struct {ref,
    max, rolloff: f32}`): `g = ref / (ref + rolloff * (d - ref))` beyond `ref` (1 inside),
    times a fade to 0 over the last 10% before `max`, 0 beyond. Classes and values: `Step
    {1, 16, 1}`, `Block {2, 24, 1}`, `Creature {2, 32, 1}`, `Player {1, 24, 1}`, `Loud {4,
    96, 0.7}` (explosions), `Ambient {4, 48, 0.5}`, `None` (UI, music: 1 always).
  - Air absorption: `cutoff = 20000 / (1 + d / 40)` Hz (at 24 blocks 12.5 kHz, at 96 about
    5.9 kHz); combined with any other cutoff by taking the lower.
  - Doppler: with `u` the unit vector from listener to emitter and `c = SPEED_OF_SOUND ::
    343` blocks/s, `pitch = (c + dot(l.velocity, u)) / (c + dot(e.velocity, u))`, clamped to
    [0.5, 2], scaled toward 1 by the setting `doppler` (0..1, default 1); multiplied into the
    voice's own pitch.
  - Direction: azimuth `az` of the emitter in the listener's frame (0 ahead, +90° right),
    elevation `el` (-90..90). Speakers: today's constant-power pan (`spatial_gain`'s
    `0.7 * min(d / 2, 1)` width), no delay, no shadow, no notch. Headphones:
    - ITD (Woodworth): `a = HEAD_RADIUS :: 0.0875` m; for the far ear `t = (a / c) * (θ +
      sin θ)` with `θ = |asin(sin az * cos el)|` (0..π/2), so at most 0.66 ms (31.5 frames
      at 48 kHz); the near ear 0.
    - Head shadow (Brown and Duda): each ear's filter `H(s) = (1 + α s / (2 ω0)) / (1 + s
      / (2 ω0))`, `ω0 = c / a`, `α(φ) = 1.05 + 0.95 cos(φ / 150° * 180°)` with `φ` the angle
      between the ear's axis (±90° azimuth) and the source, so the near ear is lifted a
      little and the far ear dulled; bilinear-transformed at the mixer's rate into
      `shadow` (the pole/zero pair is fixed by `ω0`; only α varies, which is what the
      coefficient carries).
    - Elevation: a peaking notch at `notch_hz = 8000 + 3000 * el / 90` Hz, `notch_db = -8 *
      |sin el|`, Q 3.
    - Level: both ears get the distance gain (the shadow makes the level difference), so a
      centred sound is as loud as in Speakers mode.
  - Settings: `spatial_mode` (choice Speakers / Headphones, default Headphones, help
    "Headphones: sounds are placed by small delays and filtering between the ears.
    Speakers: by level only."), `doppler` (slider 0..1, help "How much moving sounds change
    pitch.", `{{0, "Off"}}`), both on Options > Audio with tooltips.
- **Emitters in the game** (`client/emitters.odin`): a table of 128 `Emitter_Slot {voice:
  audio.Voice_Id, emitter: Emitter, follows: Follow}` (`Follow`: none, an entity id, a
  player id: then the position is updated from M50's interpolated entity each frame).
  `emitter_play(app, play: audio.Play, e: Emitter, follows := Follow{}) -> Voice_Id`;
  `emitters_update(app, pose)` once a frame recomputes every live emitter with
  `spatialize` and sends `set_voice_gain`, `set_voice_pitch`, `set_voice_cutoff`,
  `set_voice_spatial` only for those that changed by more than 0.5 dB, 1 cent, 2% or a
  frame of delay; slots free when `audio.alive` is false. `sounds_play` uses it instead
  of `spatial_gain` (the sound classes map to `Attenuation_Class`: break, place -> Block;
  step, land -> Step; hurt, die -> Creature; launch, boom -> Loud).
- **Tests** (`client/audio/spatial_test.odin`, `client/emitters_test.odin`):
  - `test_doppler`: an emitter passing at 20 b/s gives the formula's pitch within 1 cent
    at five points, and 1 at the closest point.
  - `test_head_model`: a sound at +90° in Headphones mode: the left ear's onset is 31 to 32
    frames later than the right's (cross-correlation of a click), and its energy above 4
    kHz at least 6 dB below the right's while below 300 Hz within 1 dB; at 0° both ears
    equal; elevation +45° and -45° move the notch's minimum by the formula within a third
    of an octave.
  - `test_attenuation_curves`: every class is 1 at `ref`, monotone, 0 at `max`.
  - `test_spatial_zero_is_today`: a voice with zero `Spatial` renders bit-identically to
    the M32 path; the four baselines unchanged.
  - `test_emitter_follows`: an emitter following an entity moves with it and stops being
    updated when its voice ends.

Exit check: the tests and CI green; `build bench audio` within its budget. Check by hand:
on headphones, a sheep walking round the player is heard round the head.
