# M56b: space, part two (occlusion, the room, underwater)

- **Walking a ray through blocks.** `common/game/raycast.odin` gains `Ray_Walk` (the
  Amanatides-Woo state) with `ray_walk_init(origin, dir, max_distance) -> Ray_Walk` and
  `ray_walk_next(w: ^Ray_Walk) -> (p: Block_Pos, entered: f64, ok: bool)`; `raycast` is
  rewritten on it (its tests unchanged).
- **Materials.** Each block's sound material (`Sound_Material :: enum u8 {Air, Stone, Dirt,
  Gravel, Sand, Wood, Glass, Wool, Leaves, Snow, Water, Metal, Lava}`) comes from its
  registry entry: M47's block definitions gain a `sound <material>` field if they do not
  have one (base pack: today's `sfx.material_of` mapping, with Wool and Leaves split out of
  Snow and Grass; plants and tall grass Leaves; water Water). A block shape from M48 scales
  its loss by the shape's solid fraction (a full cube 1, a slab 0.5, an open door's or a
  ladder's cell 0.1, as M48's shape API reports).
- **The acoustic table** (`client/acoustics.odin`, `ACOUSTIC :: [Sound_Material]Acoustic`,
  `Acoustic :: struct {loss_db, cutoff_factor, absorption: f32}`; loss per block passed,
  absorption of a surface for the room):

  | Material | loss_db | cutoff_factor | absorption |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Air | 0 | 1 | 1 (open) |
  | Stone | 12 | 0.25 | 0.02 |
  | Dirt | 10 | 0.30 | 0.15 |
  | Gravel | 9 | 0.35 | 0.20 |
  | Sand | 9 | 0.30 | 0.25 |
  | Wood | 8 | 0.35 | 0.10 |
  | Glass | 3 | 0.75 | 0.03 |
  | Wool | 18 | 0.12 | 0.60 |
  | Leaves | 1 | 0.90 | 0.50 |
  | Snow | 9 | 0.30 | 0.40 |
  | Water | 5 | 0.35 | 0.05 |
  | Metal | 14 | 0.25 | 0.02 |
  | Lava | 12 | 0.20 | 0.05 |

  A path's loss is the sum over the blocks it passes (each block once, whatever the length
  inside it), capped at `MAX_OCCLUSION_DB :: 36`; its cutoff `20000 * Π cutoff_factor`,
  at least 300 Hz. Unloaded chunks are air (no stall).
- **Occlusion per emitter**: the direct ray from listener to emitter (ear height) through
  `world_block`. When its loss is over 3 dB, the open-path search: 12 points around the
  emitter (±2 blocks on each axis, then ±4), in that fixed order; a point counts if both
  listener -> point and point -> emitter lose at most 3 dB; the shortest such path
  (`|LP| + |PE|`) gives a candidate with its distance gain at that length, an extra 6 dB
  and a cutoff factor 0.5 (diffraction); the louder of direct and path wins. Each emitter
  is updated every 100 ms (round-robin over the frames), and the whole is capped at
  `acoustics.rays_per_frame = 256` ray walks of at most 64 blocks; what is over waits for
  the next frame. Results are smoothed on the game side (one-pole, 80 ms) before
  `emitters_update` sends them (combined with distance and air: gains multiply, cutoffs take
  the lowest).
- **The room around the listener**: 32 fixed directions (a Fibonacci sphere, constant
  table), walked 8 per frame-batch every 62.5 ms (the full set every 0.25 s), each to at
  most 48 blocks. From the last full set: `open` = rays that escaped / 32; `sky` = escaped
  among the rays pointing up (y > 0); `mfp` = mean hit distance; `ᾱ = (Σ absorption of the
  hit materials + escaped) / 32`. Then `rt60 = clamp(0.04 * mfp / ᾱ, 0.15, 6)` s (Sabine
  with V/S = mfp / 4), `wet = 0.35 * (1 - open)^2`, `early = 0.25 * (1 - open)`, `damping =
  clamp(0.2 + ᾱ, 0.2, 0.9)`. Each is smoothed (one-pole, 0.5 s) and sent with
  `audio.set_reverb` when one moves by more than 2%. Bus sends fixed: World 0.6, Player
  0.4, Ambience 0.15, UI, Music 0.
- **Underwater and lava**: the camera's block (its sound material) Water or Lava adds
  `SNAP_UNDERWATER` (Effects 0.7 / 600 Hz, Ambience 0.6 / 400 Hz, Music 0.8 / 1200 Hz) or
  `SNAP_LAVA` (Effects 0.5 / 300 Hz, Ambience 0.5 / 300 Hz) to M44's composed snapshot,
  over 0.15 s. (No lava block exists yet: the lava case is tested through the function.)
- **Debug**: a debug variable `audio_rays` draws the last occlusion rays (green clear, red
  blocked, yellow the open path) and the room rays as debug lines; the F3 overlay's audio
  line adds `room rt60=… open=… sky=…`. The inspector (M32e) lists each voice's occlusion
  loss.
- **Cost**: the tour benchmark scenario logs `acoustics_ms` per frame; a release build
  fails the scenario when its p99 is over `acoustics.budget_ms = 0.5`.
- **Tests** (`client/acoustics_test.odin`, worlds built in the test from chunks set by
  hand):
  - `test_occlusion_by_material`: one block of stone between listener and emitter loses
    12 dB with cutoff 5 kHz; wool 18 dB; glass 3 dB; two stone 24 dB; ten stone 36 dB.
  - `test_open_path_doorway`: a wall with a 1x2 doorway 2 blocks from the direct line: the
    path is found, its loss 6 dB plus distance, louder than the wall's.
  - `test_room`: a 9x5x9 stone cave gives rt60 at least 4 times a flat field's; the field's
    wet under 0.05; ray results are identical over two runs; unloaded chunks count as open.
  - `test_underwater_snapshot`: the camera in water gives the snapshot; out, neutral.
  - `test_space_walk`: a built world (a field, a stone hill with a 3x3 tunnel 20 blocks
    long): the listener walks from the field through the mouth to the tunnel's middle in 20
    steps; rt60 never falls and an emitter left in the field never gets brighter (cutoff
    non-increasing). A new audio baseline scenario `space_walk` renders it (footsteps and a
    sheep left in the field) and is accepted.

Exit check: the tests and CI green; the tour scenario within its acoustics budget;
`build audio --wav build/audio space_walk` writes the walk. Check by hand: listen to the walk;
look once at the `audio_rays` lines (a screenshot in the report).
