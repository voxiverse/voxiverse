# M57b: sound design, part two (the new sounds, the listening tools, references)

- **The sounds M58 needs**, as recipes with their target rows (first estimates): `water
  splash` (bubbles and noise, 0.3..1.0 s, centroid 800..3000), `water swim` (soft
  bubbles, 0.2..0.5 s, -30..-20 LUFS), `water flow` loop (8 s, `loop 1`), `armor equip`
  (metal and cloth, 0.15..0.4 s), `item pickup` (a rising tone pair, 0.05..0.2 s, centroid
  1500..5000), `inventory click` (0.01..0.06 s, -36..-26 LUFS), `ui click`, `ui back`,
  `ui tab` (0.01..0.08 s, centroid 1500..6000, -36..-26 LUFS, `character clean`), `chest
  open` and `chest close` (wood creak: modal wood with a slow granular rasp, 0.3..0.8 s),
  `wool` break/place/step if not already distinct, `gate dial` and `gate open` (the gate's
  chevron lock and the horizon's whoosh). Ambience beds are M58b's.
- **Tools** (`tools/sfx`; `"sfx"` joins `ensure_miniaudio`'s list since it now plays sound):
  - `build sfx --spectrograms`: a PNG per sound (`audio.spectrogram_rgba`) beside its WAVs.
  - `build sfx --listen <class|name>`: plays each variant in turn through the default
    device, 0.6 s apart, printing its measures; Enter repeats, `q` ends.
  - `build sfx --compare <git rev>`: in `build/worktrees/<hash>` (the bench compare's
    worktrees, reused) runs that tree's `build sfx`, then plays old and new of a class
    alternately, level-matched (each scaled to -23 LUFS momentary max).
  - `build sfx --abx <class> [--rev <git rev>]`: a blind ABX test of old against new
    (level-matched; X random each trial by `base.Rng` seeded from the time; 10 trials;
    keys a, b, x to hear, 1 or 2 to answer); answers appended to
    `build/sounds/abx.log` with the class, the score and the binomial p.
  - `build sfx --analyze <wav> [--class <class>]`: measures a WAV on the designer's
    machine (a reference they chose; never committed) and prints a targets row in
    `targets.txt`'s format to paste: the only way reference recordings reach the repo is as
    these numbers.
- **Tests**: the new sounds pass the M57 tests (targets, clicks, variants, determinism);
  `--analyze` of a WAV the test writes (a known decaying tone) prints the expected row;
  the ABX scorer's binomial p for 9 of 10 is 0.011.

Exit check: the tests and CI green; `build sfx` exits 0. Check by hand: `--listen` every
class, `--abx` the ones in doubt, and send back which to retune (an assistant cannot hear:
measurements keep the sounds correct, ears decide whether they are good).
