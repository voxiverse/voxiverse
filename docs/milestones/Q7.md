# Q7: the soak (a long server run with bots building, fighting, coming and going, and scripts)

**Why.** The owner (#84): a server under a long, varied load keeps its memory and tick time
flat, never crashes, and leaves saves that are valid. M55e's load test proves 200 bots for
ten minutes at one behaviour; a leak of a few bytes a join, a pool that never shrinks, a
script heap that creeps, or a save that goes wrong after its hundredth autosave shows only
over hours and over churn. This row runs that: nightly for an hour, and three minutes on
every push.

**Placed after M55e** (by dependency): it is built on M54b's bot harness (`bot/`:
`Bot`, `bot_pump`, `bot_step`, the verbs, `Behaviour`), M55e's load harness (the bot
process `voxiverse-server --bots`, `--stats-out`, `build load`'s universe setup and its
`result.json`), the sandbox (M52, M52b: scripts with capabilities and budgets), the
declarative layer (M53, M53b: a quest, a spawner, a loot table), M45's journal and replay,
M31's ledger and `audit`, M46's `--check` (`dedicated/main.odin`'s `run_check`), M48g's
damage, death and respawn and M48h's weapons, and Q3's crash bundles (a bundle written is a
crash).

**Checked against the code first.** This spec is written before M54b, M55e, M52 and M53 are
built; the session checks the names above against what they built and corrects this spec in
the same commit, as the plan's rule says, without reopening the decisions below.

**The soak** (`build soak`, `tools/build/soak.odin`; the bot behaviours in `bot/`):

- `build soak [--minutes N] [--bots N] [--seed N] [--out <dir>]`: defaults 60 minutes, 64
  bots, seed 84, `build/soak/`.
- The universe: fresh in `<out>/universe`, made as `build load` makes its universe (four
  worlds, the `bot` role), plus the soak pack `testdata/packs/soak/` (new, written by this
  row, built only from what packs and scripts can do): a server script that, on a timer every
  10 s, places and clears a 5x5 platform in its granted region, spawns up to 8 sheep and
  removes them after 60 s, and gives a bot that enters its region one item through its
  faucet; a quest with two objectives (break 10 stone, reach a point) and a loot table; a
  spawner of sheep near each world's spawn. Grants: the pack's capabilities in the home world
  and one other.
- Survival with pvp on in two of the four worlds (`/rule` at setup, as the party bench does),
  creative in the others, so fighting, dying and respawning happen.
- The bots (one process, M55e's), behaviours assigned round-robin by bot number:
  - `builder` (new, `bot/builder.odin`): builds and breaks a 4x4x4 hollow cube in its own
    plot (a plot per bot, 16 blocks apart along a line from spawn), placing and breaking
    one block a second;
  - `fighter` (new, `bot/fighter.odin`): in a pvp world, walks to the nearest other
    fighter, attacks it and fires at it with the mode's kit, respawns when it dies;
  - `quester` (M54j's `explorer`): takes and follows the soak pack's quest;
  - `wanderer` (M55e's `wander`), including its travel between worlds;
  - `churner` (new, `bot/churner.odin`): plays as a wanderer for a span drawn from 30 to 180
    s, leaves (a clean disconnect half the time, a dropped link without goodbye the other
    half), and joins again 5 to 20 s later, as the same player two times in three and as a
    new name the third.
- Every 5 minutes the tool sends `/save` (the console of the server process); at the end it
  stops the server cleanly.
- Sampling: the server's `--stats-out` lines (M55e's, every 10 s) plus, added here, its
  peak and current resident memory (`peak_resident_memory`, `resident_memory`), the session
  arena's used bytes, each pool's live count (entities, chunks loaded, clients, block
  entities, scripts' VM heap bytes summed), the journal's open segment count, and error and
  fatal log lines counted.

**The verdicts** (`<out>/result.json` as `build load` writes it, and `<out>/report.md` with
a table per minute), measured after a warm-up (10 minutes nightly; 1 minute in the push
run). A soak passes when every one holds; a failure is a bug, fixed at its cause, never a
threshold loosened without the owner:

- **Memory flat:** the highest resident memory in the last third of the measured span is at
  most the highest of the middle third times 1.05 plus 8 MiB; the session arena's used
  bytes and each pool's live count, sampled at the same moments (bots count and worlds
  loaded are equal there by construction: churn is periodic), the same rule.
- **Tick time flat:** the median of the per-minute tick p99 over the last third is at most
  that of the first measured third times 1.10; and M55e's budget holds throughout (tick p99
  ≤ 33.3 ms in every 10 s window).
- **Nothing crashes:** the server and the bot process exit 0; no crash bundle (Q3) exists
  under `<out>/universe/crashes/`; zero `fatal` lines and zero `error` lines in the server's
  log (a script's own errors are logged as `warn` by the sandbox, M52, so they do not count;
  a sandbox `error` does).
- **No unexplained disconnects:** every disconnect in the bot report is a churner's own.
- **Saves valid:** after the clean stop, `voxiverse-server --check --universe <out>/universe`
  reports no problem; the universe opens again (a fresh server starts on it, every world
  loads, it stops); the journal replays from the start to the same state hash as the stopped
  server's (M45's replay; for the worlds whose journal starts at creation); the ledger's
  `audit` has no lines (M31: every item made or destroyed accounted for, script gives
  included).

**CI:**

- "Soak (short)" on every push: a job of its own in `ci.yml` (`needs: linux-tests`,
  ubuntu-latest, timeout 15 minutes): `./build.sh soak --minutes 4 --bots 16` (fixed seed
  84), uploading `build/soak/` as an artifact when it fails.
- "Soak · nightly" in `nightly.yml` (Q1's file; ubuntu-latest, timeout 120 minutes):
  `./build.sh soak --minutes 75 --bots 64 --seed ${{ github.run_number }}` (60 measured after
  15 of warm-up and joining), uploading `build/soak/` always (its report is the evidence).
- Q5's `docs/quality.md` gains the line "A server under bots for an hour: memory and tick
  time flat, no crash, saves valid" enforced by `ci:Soak · nightly` and `ci:Soak (short)`.

**Tests** (in the suite, short, no timing asserted, T5):

- `bot/`: each new behaviour on T2's stepped harness for 600 ticks does what it says
  (the builder's cube exists then is gone; two fighters damage each other and one respawns;
  a churner leaves and rejoins, as the same key and as a new one).
- `tools/build`: the verdicts on recorded sample series (a flat one passes; a growing
  memory, a growing tick, a crash bundle, an error line, a foreign disconnect and a failed
  `--check` each fail with the reason named).
- The soak pack loads with no `Pack_Error` and its script passes M54g's static validator.

**Steps, in order:** the three behaviours and their tests; the soak pack; the samples added to
`--stats-out`; `build soak` and its verdicts with their tests; a local 20-minute run, fixing
what it finds; the CI jobs; the quality line.

**Left out:** client soak (a game client left running for hours: the renderer's memory is
checked by R9's rows; a client soak waits for a headless renderer path); soak on Windows and
macOS (the server code is the same; Linux's runner is CI's); a soak of more than 64 bots
(M55e's load test owns scale).

**Open questions** (none blocks this row): none.

**Exit check:** `./build.sh soak --minutes 20 --bots 32` passes locally (its report quoted in
the session's report: verdicts and the per-minute memory and tick columns, no machine
details); the push job green in CI; the suite, `build check` and CI green.

**Check by hand:** none.
