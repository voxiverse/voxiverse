# R11c: the look, part two (water's reflection and depth, foliage in the wind)

The second row of the owner's look ([R11b](R11b.md), R11c; split by #87). Arc rules:
[R.md](R.md#rules-for-every-r-milestone). The owner's decisions (2026-10-30) are kept as
written; R11b's look file, toggles, `plain/` baselines, `--check look` and `Look` CI step are
extended, not repeated.

**Why.** Water today is a texture blended at its texture's alpha (`water.frag`): it reflects
nothing and looks the same over a puddle and over a trench. Plants and leaves stand still.

**Checked against the code (2026-10-30, #87; corrections to the old R11b text):**

- **The water's depth behind it needs the depth image while water is drawn.** Water is drawn
  in R11's one graphics pass after the sky, depth-tested without writing. Sampling the depth
  image there is legal only in a read-only depth layout: the water becomes **its own graph
  pass** (`water`: depth `.Load`, not written, also sampled, layout
  `DEPTH_READ_ONLY_OPTIMAL`), between the pass that ends with the sky and the one with the debug
  lines and the view model. The graph derives the two transitions.
- **Which blocks sway.** The owner named grass and leaves. The base pack's grass plant is
  `tall_grass` (`render: "cross"`, `data/blocks/plants.json`); the grass *block* is a terrain
  cube and does not sway. Leaves are `leaves` (`data/blocks/trees.json`). The other cross
  plants (`poppy`, `dandelion`, `wheat_0`-`wheat_7`) are an open question. The mechanism is a
  block tag (`data/block_tags/sways.json`, the registry's `Block_Tag` kind, which packs already
  use), so a pack's own plants can sway without code.
- **A swaying vertex leaves its chunk's box.** R6's frustum test and R7's occlusion test use
  the chunk's 16-block box; a leaf at the border moves up to the sway amount outside it. The
  cull pass's box grows by the largest sway (a push constant from the look), so no culled chunk
  had a visible vertex (the conservativeness R7 proved stays proved).
- **The wind's time.** "The server's time (the world tick, not the client clock)": the client
  carries the world's clock (`Session.clock`, the server's last word carried on); the scene
  gains `wind_time = clock` (wrapped to keep `f32` precision: modulo the wind's period,
  computed in `f64` on the CPU), so two clients at the same world time draw the same sway.
- **R8's quad record** has spare bits: the `sways` bit goes in word `a`'s spare bit, set by the
  mesher from the block's tag.

## Design (R11c)

### Water (`water.frag`, `assets/shaders/water.glsl`)

- **Reflection of the sky** (from above only: with the camera under water, `Scene.water.w`,
  the surface keeps R11b's look): the view direction reflected about the surface's normal (up,
  for a top face; the side faces' own normals), `sky_color(scene, reflected)` (the sky model's
  zenith, horizon and glow; no screen-space trace, no clouds in it), mixed in by Schlick's
  fresnel `F = f0 + (1 - f0) * (1 - cos)^power` scaled by `water.fresnel_strength`, with
  `f0 = 0.02` (water's index of refraction) and `power` the tunable `water.fresnel_power` (5 is
  Schlick's).
- **Depth fade:** the distance through water to what is behind it, from the depth image
  (reverse Z, infinite: view distance `= near / depth`) minus the surface's own; opacity rises
  and colour moves toward `Scene.water.rgb` as `1 - exp(-distance / water.fade_distance)`;
  where nothing is behind within the view (the sky at depth 0), the fade is complete.
- Tunables in `assets/look.json`: `water.fresnel_strength`, `water.fresnel_power`,
  `water.fade_distance` (blocks). Toggle `Look_Effect.Water`: off is R11b's water exactly.

### Foliage (`world.vert`, `assets/shaders/wind.glsl`)

- `wind_offset(world_pos, time, amount, speed) -> vec3` (horizontal only): two octaves of a
  hash-based value noise of the block's world position (integer cell, so a block's corners
  shared with a neighbour move together: no cracks between leaf blocks) advanced by
  `time * speed`; a cross plant's bottom corners (`v` = 0 in the quad) stay put, its top corners
  move by the offset; a leaf block's corners all move by the offset at their own position.
- Positions are relative to the camera's chunk; the noise is taken at the absolute cell (chunk
  * 16 + local, integers) so it is continuous across chunk borders and exact far from the
  origin.
- Tunables: `foliage.amount` (blocks, at most 0.25), `foliage.speed`. Reduce motion sets the
  amount to 0. Toggle `Look_Effect.Foliage`: off is no offset.
- `wind_reference` (CPU, `client/render/look.odin`) from the same constants (rule 9).

## Tests (R11c)

- `client/render/look_test.odin`:
  - `test_wind_is_shared`: `wind_reference` at the same world time and cell is the same in two
    `Session`s built from two clients' clocks of a stepped server (the same tick); different
    cells differ; zero under Reduce motion and with the toggle off.
  - `test_wind_has_no_cracks`: the offsets of a leaf block's eight corners equal those of its
    six neighbours' coinciding corners (the same function of the same cell corner).
  - `test_fresnel_reference`: at normal incidence the reflection weight is `f0 *
    fresnel_strength`, at grazing it tends to `fresnel_strength`; the depth fade is 0 at no
    depth and tends to 1.
  - `test_sways_tag`: the base pack's `sways` tag holds `tall_grass` and `leaves` (and what the
    owner adds); the mesher sets the record's bit for exactly those.
- `--check look` (extended): `wind.glsl` against `wind_reference` on 4 096 cells and times
  within 1e-5 block; `water.glsl`'s fresnel and fade against their references on a grid, within
  1/4096.
- `--check cull` (extended): with a sway margin, a chunk whose box is just outside the
  frustum but whose margin is inside is kept; R7's `test_occlusion_is_conservative` with the
  margin.
- `images`: the default set re-accepted for the scenes that change (water: `water`,
  `underwater`; foliage: the scenes showing plants or leaves) and still catching a change (a
  mutation of `water.fresnel_strength` and of `foliage.amount` fails them); the look-off set
  equal to `plain/`, with no re-accept.
- `--check occlusion` (R7b) with foliage on: no false occlusion (the margin).

## Benchmarks (R11c)

Each effect its own commit: `bench compare <its parent> views` and `views -size:2560x1440`,
reported; `gpu_water` for the water effect, `gpu_world` for foliage. **The look's budget** (the
owner's rule, R11b): the six effects together at most 15% more `gpu_frame` than R11's last
commit until measured; at the end of this row it is replaced by the measured figure plus 25%
headroom, written in the history entry and in docs/debugging.md's cost table.

## Left out (R11c)

Screen-space reflections, refraction, caustics, foam, water waves beyond R8's swell; wind
from the weather; swaying of anything not in the tag (vines, crops) until the owner names it;
bloom, light shafts, vignette, film grain, sun shadows, anti-aliasing (the owner's choice).

## Exit check (R11c)

**Gated in CI:** `--check look` (water and wind parts), `--check cull` with the margin and
`--check occlusion` with foliage on, all on lavapipe under validation; the look tests;
`images` both sets (re-accepted where it changed and catching mutations; look-off equal to
R11's); the `Look` step's screenshots; `build check` and the suite. **Measured** (development
machine, reported): each effect's cost; the six together within the look's budget.
