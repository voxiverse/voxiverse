# R13: the level-of-detail mesher (downsampled chunks with skirts; built and tested, not drawn yet)

The first of two level-of-detail rows (R13, [R13b](R13b.md); split by #87: the mesher, its
watertightness test and its budget are one session, choosing levels in the world and
swapping them without holes another). Arc rules: [R.md](R.md#rules-for-every-r-milestone).

**Why.** Every chunk out to the view radius is a full-detail mesh, so the view distance cannot
grow without its cost growing with radius² x height. Far chunks can be meshed from a coarser
grid of the blocks the client already has (no server change: R14's far columns are another,
held row).

**Checked against the code (2026-10-30, #87; corrections to the old R13 text):**

- **The view radius that benchmarks and `images` use is 12** (`BENCHMARK_SETTINGS.render_distance`),
  so with `lod_start` 8 (the old text's default) the horizon of `north`, `east`, `south`,
  `west` and of `views` changes too, not only two new far scenes. R13b re-accepts the scenes
  that change and lists them for a look by hand; the old "two far-view scenes" stay as R13b's
  additions.
- A mesh job reads an 18³ padded block snapshot and the chunk's cached light (R4's
  `Light_Cache`: `mesh.Light_Snapshot`, 18³ cells of `sky << 4 | block`). The padding is one
  block: a coarse cell across a chunk border is judged from that one-block layer (below),
  and the skirts cover what that approximation misses.
- R8's quad record holds positions and extents in sixteenths up to 511, so a level-2 cell (32
  sixteenths) and a level-4 cell (64) need no new record; textures repeat with the world grid,
  so a 2-block face shows its texture twice (blocky, as the aesthetic wants: no new texture
  work).
- A mesh's layers, face groups and counts stay as they are (R8), so culling, occlusion and
  water's sort need nothing new for a coarse mesh.

## Design (R13)

### Downsampling (`client/mesh/lod.odin`)

`lod_grid(s: ^Snapshot, light: ^Light_Snapshot, reg, level: Lod_Level) -> Lod_Grid` with
`Lod_Level` `.L2` (2 x 2 x 2 blocks a cell, 8³ cells) and `.L4` (4 x 4 x 4, 4³ cells):

- **The block of a cell** (deterministic, the same on every machine): the cell's blocks are
  counted by kind; if at least half the cell is opaque, the most common opaque block; else if
  at least half is cutout or opaque, the most common cutout block; else if at least half is
  water, water at the cell's highest level; else air. Ties go to the lower block id. Shapes
  (M48: stairs, slabs) count as the block whose texture they show, as a full cube.
- **The light of a cell**: the maximum sky and the maximum block light over its non-opaque
  blocks (a cell lit through any opening reads as lit: a far valley does not go black), or the
  maximum over its face-adjacent padded neighbours when the cell is all opaque.
- **The border**: a coarse cell beyond the chunk's face is solid when the padded layer's
  blocks it touches are at least half opaque (one block deep, not the cell's full depth).

### Meshing (`mesh_lod(reg, grid, level, out) -> Counts`)

Faces between a solid coarse cell and a non-solid one, in the same layers and face groups as
`mesh_chunk`, through the same greedy merge (R8's packing after it), AO from the coarse grid
(the same rule on cells as on blocks), cross plants left out (a far plant is nothing), water's
surface as a flat coarse face at the cell's level. The output is `mesh.Vertex` quads like
`mesh_chunk`'s, so R8's packing and every later step take it unchanged.

### Skirts

On each of the chunk's four side borders, below every border column's exposed top face, a
skirt: a quad on the border plane facing outward, from that top face down by `skirt_depth =
2^level` blocks (clamped to the chunk's bottom; continued by the chunk below's own skirt when
its top is also exposed there), in the column's block's texture, opaque layer, its own face
group (so back-face culling hides it from inside). A skirt sits exactly where a finer
neighbour's surface may be lower than the coarse one; from outside, a crack between levels
shows the skirt, not the sky. Skirts are only on coarse meshes (full-detail meshes have none).

## The order of the steps (R13)

1. `lod_grid` and its tests.
2. `mesh_lod` and its tests; R8's round trip over its quads.
3. Skirts; the watertightness test.
4. The budget scene and counts in `build bench chunks`; docs (debugging.md: what a coarse
   mesh is); this row's history entry.

## Tests (R13)

- `client/mesh/lod_test.odin`:
  - `test_lod_cell_block`: by hand, a cell of each rule (half opaque, a tie, leaves over air,
    water levels, a slab) gives the stated block; the same grid twice gives the same result.
  - `test_lod_cell_light`: an enclosed air pocket keeps its light; an opaque cell beside a
    lit one is lit.
  - `test_lod_flat_ground`: a flat stone floor at y 0 to 7 gives, at `.L2`, one merged top
    face of 16 x 16 blocks and the four skirts, nothing else.
  - `test_lod_mesh_round_trips`: every quad from `mesh_lod` over `build bench chunks`' region at
    both levels packs into R8's record and expands back exactly.
  - `test_lod_is_watertight` (property, fixed seed 87, 300 regions): a 3 x 1 x 3 neighbourhood
    of chunks from the scratch world's generator at random positions, the centre chunk at full
    detail and each neighbour at a random level; from 64 random cameras outside the terrain,
    rays toward the centre chunk's borders are cast on the CPU against the full-detail mesh of
    all nine and against the mixed-level meshes: every ray that hits terrain in the full-detail
    world must hit something in the mixed one within `2^level + 1` blocks of the same distance
    (a hole is a ray that reaches the sky or a far wall instead). It fails without skirts (tried
    by hand, said in the report).
- `build bench chunks` (`tools/bench/main.odin`): `Counts` gains `lod_quads[level]`; `CHECK`
  gains them exact for the default region, and `budget_ms` gains `{.Mesh_Lod, <set from the
  first run>}` (`Metric.Mesh_Lod`: `lod_grid` plus `mesh_lod` per chunk), checked by `-check`
  in CI as the other stages are; the default region's `lod_quads[.L2]` at most 30% of its full
  quads (else the downsample does not pay; a failed bound is a design problem reported, never
  a moved bound).
