# R13b: far terrain at level of detail in the world

The second level-of-detail row ([R13](R13.md), R13b; split by #87). Arc rules:
[R.md](R.md#rules-for-every-r-milestone).

**Why.** R13 built the coarse mesher and proved its seams covered. This row picks a level per
chunk by its distance, meshes far chunks coarsely, and swaps levels without a frame that shows
a hole, so far terrain costs a fraction of near terrain.

**What this builds on:** R13's `lod_grid`, `mesh_lod`, skirts; the client's meshing
(`client/world.odin`: `Client_Chunk`, the mesh queue `to_mesh`, `Mesh_Task`, `task_take`,
`replace_mesh`, mesh groups (`Mesh_Group`, `groups_update`, `world_show_mesh`) that show a bulk
edit's meshes in one frame); R7b's culling and `--check occlusion`; R8-R10's arena, upload and
`in_flight` rule; R11's graph; the scene counts.

**Checked against the code (2026-10-30, #87; corrections to the old R13 text):**

- "Level changes swap meshes in one frame (as mesh groups do)": mesh groups exist for bulk
  edits (`Mesh_Group`, shown once all of a group's chunks have their new meshes). A level
  change is a group of one chunk: its new mesh is added hidden and shown in the frame its old
  one is removed (`replace_mesh`), and with R10 only once uploaded. So a level change never
  shows a hole by construction; R13b's checks test it rather than build a new mechanism.
- "`views-far` `gpu_world` at most 60% of the same scenario with LOD disabled (`debug.lod 0`)":
  `bench compare` compares commits, not switches, so the comparison is against **the commit
  before R13b** (no LOD) on `views-far`, which is the same thing; `debug.lod 0` exists for
  hand checks and for `--check lod`'s own comparison.
- The scenes at radius 12 change beyond `lod_start` (8): R13's correction; they are listed
  and re-accepted here.
- A chunk edited far away (a far explosion, water flowing) is re-meshed at its level: the light
  cache path (R4) and the mesh queue are unchanged, only the mesher differs.

## Design (R13b)

### Levels

- `Settings.lod_start` (chunks, default 8, the old text's decision; 0 turns level of detail
  off; range 4 to 32), a client setting in the settings table (saved, `set lod_start` in the
  console) but **not in Options** until the owner answers (below), and `debug.lod` (0 off, 1
  on; development builds) for comparisons.
- A chunk's level from its horizontal distance in chunks to the camera's chunk (the
  interest cylinder's own measure): full detail below `lod_start`, `.L2` from `lod_start`,
  `.L4` from `2 * lod_start`. **Hysteresis:** a chunk changes level only when it is a whole
  chunk past the boundary in the direction of the change (so crossing a boundary back and
  forth re-meshes nothing).
- `Client_Chunk.level` (the level of its shown mesh) and `want` (the level its distance asks
  for); a chunk whose `want` differs from `level` is queued for meshing at `want`, behind
  chunks that have no mesh and behind edited chunks (a level change is the least urgent work);
  `Mesh_Task.level` makes the job call `mesh_lod` instead of `mesh_chunk`.
- When the camera moves, levels are reviewed for the chunks whose distance band changed (the
  ring of chunks crossing a boundary, found from the camera's chunk delta, not a walk of every
  chunk).

### The swap

The new mesh replaces the old one in one frame through `replace_mesh` (hidden until uploaded,
R10). The cull pass's records carry no level; nothing else in the renderer changes.

### Metrics, counts and the overlay

`lod_meshes` (`Metric.Lod_Meshes`, per frame: meshes shown at `.L2` and `.L4`), `lod_remesh`
(level changes a second); scene counts gain `lod_meshes` (exact); F3's `render:` line shows
them; `debug.chunk_borders` colours a coarse chunk's outline by its level.

### Scenes

`far_north: /tp 0 120 0 0 -10; set render_distance 24` and `far_down: /tp 0 160 0 30 -45; set
render_distance 24` (radius 24, height from the view setting), accepted in this row; the
existing scenes whose pictures change beyond `lod_start` re-accepted, each listed in the report
with its `lod_meshes` count (the others must pass unchanged).

## The order of the steps (R13b)

1. `lod_meshes` in the scene counts on the code before LOD (0 everywhere), and the two far
   scenes accepted **without LOD** (one commit: their no-LOD baselines are kept as
   `plain/far_north.png` and `plain/far_down.png` beside R11b's plain set, rendered with
   `debug.lod 0`, so `build images` keeps checking the no-LOD picture too).
2. Levels, hysteresis, the queue, the job's mesher by level.
3. The setting, the overlay; the scenes re-accepted with LOD.
4. `--check lod`, the CI step; measurement; docs; history.md (R13 and R13b's specs appended).

## Tests (R13b)

- `client/world_test.odin` (stepped, no GPU, with the fake pass of R9b's tests):
  - `test_lod_levels_by_distance`: levels for a ring of chunks at `lod_start` 8: full, `.L2`,
    `.L4` at the stated distances; `lod_start` 0 is full everywhere.
  - `test_lod_hysteresis`: a camera stepping back and forth across a boundary re-meshes
    nothing; a whole chunk past it re-meshes the band once.
  - `test_lod_swap_has_no_gap`: across a level change, every frame has exactly one mesh shown
    for the chunk (the old until the frame the new one is uploaded, then the new).
  - `test_lod_queue_order`: an unmeshed chunk and an edited chunk are meshed before any level
    change.
- `--check lod` (`client/lod_check.odin`, a CI step on lavapipe under validation): at radius
  24, fly the `fly` check's route at 40 blocks/s with `debug.occlusion_verify 1` (R7b): no
  false occlusion (coarse meshes and skirts are culled by the same box); at the route's end,
  two still frames of a fixed far view with `debug.lod 1` and `debug.lod 0`: `world_triangles`
  with LOD at most 40% of without (the old exit check's triangle half, gated here because it is
  a count), and every pixel below the horizon line in the no-LOD frame (terrain, from its
  depth: depth above zero) is also terrain in the LOD frame (depth above zero): **no hole**, the
  picture-level watertightness check, R13's property test in the real world.
- `images`: the far scenes against both their baselines (`plain/` without LOD, the default
  with); the re-accepted scenes; counts with `lod_meshes`.

## Left out (R13b)

Morphing between levels (a swap is instant, its seam a skirt); levels chosen by screen-space
error instead of distance; a coarse mesh across several chunks; far columns beyond the
interest area (R14, held); entities at a level of detail.

## Exit check (R13b)

**Gated in CI:** `--check lod` (no false occlusion over the route; triangles at most 40%;
no terrain pixel without LOD that is sky with it) under validation; the world tests; `images`
with the far scenes against both sets and the re-accepted scenes; `build check` and the
suite. **Measured** (development machine, reported): `views-far` `gpu_world` at most 60% of
the commit before R13b's.
