# R15: entities and held items on the GPU (models uploaded once, instances per frame)

The first of two rows (R15, [R15b](R15b.md); split by #87: entity boxes and held items are
one session, M50d's moving bodies on the world pass another). Comes after M50's entity
storage (the order of 2026-10-30) and M50d. Arc rules: [R.md](R.md#rules-for-every-r-milestone).

**Why.** Entity boxes and held models are turned into vertices on the CPU every frame
(`client/render/entity_pass.odin`: `entity_upload` writes 24 vertices of 28 bytes per box;
`held_pass.odin`: `held_world_add` appends every vertex of every third-person item, and
`view_model_add` every vertex of the view model, moved by a matrix on the CPU). Cheap with a
few players; it grows with the number of entities and the size of their models.

**Checked against the code (2026-10-30, #87; corrections to the old R15 text):**

- **What `avatar_draw` measures.** The `avatars` scenario's `Avatar_Draw` scope wraps
  `bench_avatars_draw` → `avatar_draw`, which appends `Entity_Box` structs (cheap) and, for
  each held weapon, every vertex of its `Held_Mesh` through `held_world_add` (the cost: a
  voxel gun is hundreds to thousands of vertices). The box-to-vertex expansion is in
  `entity_upload`, outside that scope. So the old target (`avatar_draw` at most 25%) is met by
  moving held models to instances; the boxes' expansion moving to the GPU shows in
  `frame_record` (also measured).
- **The model sources.** Held items are `Held_Mesh` (`client/viewmodel_mesh.odin`: triangles
  of `render.Held_Vertex`, built per item kind and cached in `Held_Cache`); boxes are
  `Entity_Box {pivot f64, yaw, pitch, lo, hi, color, part}`, built per frame by
  `avatars.odin` and `entities.odin` (players, pack kinds drawn by M50e's `looks`, dropped
  stacks, projectiles, particles: `Puff`). Boxes have no shared model: each is a pivot, two
  angles and a box, so a box is an **instance of a unit cube**, not of an uploaded box set.
- **The view model** is drawn with its own scene after a depth clear (`held_draw_view`); the
  third-person items with the world's (`held_draw_world`). Both stay; each becomes one draw.
- The preview (`client/preview.odin`, the player in the inventory) draws avatars through the
  same passes into `gpu.Target`: it goes along.
- Visual baselines show held items (`held_first`, `held_third`) but no entity: a crowd scene
  is added before anything changes (below), so the boxes' move is gated too.

## Design (R15)

### Boxes as instances (`entity.vert`)

`Entity_Box` becomes the GPU's instance record, 64 bytes: the pivot as the camera chunk's
offset in `f32` computed on the CPU from the `f64` pivot (as `entity_upload` does now, so
precision is unchanged), yaw, pitch, `lo`, `hi`, colour, part. `entity_upload` copies the
records into the frame slot's storage buffer (no expansion); `entity.vert` makes a box's 24
corners (36 vertices, `gl_VertexIndex` over a constant corner and face table) from the record
at `gl_InstanceIndex`, turning them as `entity_box`'s CPU code does (yaw, then pitch, about the
pivot), with each face's shade and uv as today. One `vkCmdDraw(36, boxes, 0, 0)`.

### Models (`client/render/models.odin`)

- `Model_Library`: a device-local buffer of `Held_Vertex` (R9's allocator, a
  `base.Range_Allocator` inside it, growing as R9b's arena does), filled once per item kind
  when `Held_Cache` first builds its mesh (uploaded through R10's ring) and freed when the cache
  drops it (a pack reload); `Model_Handle` (`base.Pool`) holds its range.
- `Model_Instance` (64 bytes): the model's first vertex and count, a 3 x 4 transform relative to
  the camera's chunk (built on the CPU from the `f64` base and the hand's pose, as
  `held_world_add`'s callers compute it now), the light (sky and block, as the batch has it).
- Per frame and slot: an instance buffer for the world's items and one for the view model;
  one `vk.DrawIndirectCommand` per instance (`vertexCount` the model's, `firstVertex` its first,
  `firstInstance` the instance), written by the CPU into a host-visible indirect buffer; one
  `vkCmdDrawIndirect` per list (`held.vert` reads its instance by `gl_InstanceIndex` through
  `firstInstance`, its vertex from the library by `gl_VertexIndex`).
- `view_model_add` and `held_world_add` become `held_instance_add(list, model, transform,
  light)`; the arm is a model too (`held_mesh_arm` uploaded once per colour in use).

### CPU twins (rule 9)

`entity_vertices_reference` (today's `entity_upload` expansion, kept as the reference) and
`held_vertices_reference` (today's CPU transform). `--check models` (`client/render/
models_check.odin`, a CI step on lavapipe under validation): a compute shader including
`entity_box.glsl` and `held_transform.glsl` (the functions `entity.vert` and `held.vert`
call) expands 4 096 generated boxes (random pivots up to 30 000 blocks from the origin, angles,
sizes, parts) and every model of the base pack's items at 64 random transforms; every position
within 1e-4 block of the reference (the same `f32` operations in a different order), every
uv, shade, colour and part equal.

## The order of the steps (R15)

1. A `crowd` scene (`images`): `set debug.crowd 8` (a development switch that draws the
   `avatars` scenario's crowd through `bench_avatars_draw`'s code, standing still at stride 0)
   and `look 0 -10`; accepted on the code as it is, with counts.
2. Boxes as instances; `entity_vertices_reference`; `images` unchanged.
3. The model library, instances and indirect draws for third-person items and the view model;
   `held_vertices_reference`; `images` unchanged.
4. `--check models`, the CI step; measurement; docs (debugging.md: entities and held items,
   how a pack's model reaches the GPU); history.md.

## Tests (R15)

- `client/render/render_test.odin`: `test_entity_box_corners` (a box's corners by hand at yaw
  90 and pitch 45; the reference and the shader table agree on the face order);
  `test_model_library_reuse` (a kind's model uploaded once across frames and items; freed with
  its cache; a pack reload re-uploads).
- `client/viewmodel_test.odin`: the view model's instance transform equals the old vertex
  transform for the start kit's launcher and the arm (the reference).
- `--check models` (CI).
- `images`: `crowd`, `held_first`, `held_third` and the rest unchanged, with counts
  (`frame_triangles` and `draw_calls` change knowingly: re-accepted counts only, said in the
  report).
- `build bench viewmodel` (T5's budget): its frame becomes building one instance; the budget
  `BENCH_VIEWMODEL_BUDGET_US` (100) stays and is now met by a wide margin (reported).

## Benchmarks (R15)

`bench compare <the commit before R15> avatars`, `views`:

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| `avatars` `avatar_draw` | at most 25% of the commit before R15's (p < 0.05): the old exit check |
| `avatars` `frame_record` | lower (the boxes' expansion gone from the CPU) |
| `avatars` `gpu_entities`, `gpu_held` | within noise or better |
| `views` `gpu_frame` | within noise |

## Left out (R15)

Moving bodies (R15b); culling entities and held items on the GPU (tens of them: the CPU's
frustum test where it exists is kept); occlusion of entities; skinning (limbs are boxes);
entity models from packs beyond boxes and held item meshes (M50e's `looks` choose among the
engine's).

## Exit check (R15)

**Gated in CI:** `--check models` passes on lavapipe under validation; the tests; `images`
with `crowd`, `held_first` and `held_third` unchanged; `--benchmark viewmodel` within its
budget in CI's Profiling step; `build check` and the suite. **Measured** (development
machine, reported): `avatars` `avatar_draw` at most 25% of the commit before R15's.
