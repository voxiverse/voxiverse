# R15b: moving bodies on the world pass (quad records with a pose, culled and occluded on the GPU)

The second of two rows ([R15](R15.md), R15b; split by #87). After M50d (moving block
structures drawn by the held pass) and R15. Arc rules: [R.md](R.md#rules-for-every-r-milestone).

**Why.** M50d draws a body (a door, a lift, a vehicle: up to 32 cells a side, meshed as up to
eight 16³ pieces by `mesh_chunk` once per revision) through the held pass's world path: its
vertices transformed by a model matrix in `Held_Push` (`held.vert` with a yaw), culled by their
bounds on the CPU (at most 64 bodies a world), and not part of the world's culling,
occlusion, compact geometry or upload path. A body is world geometry that moves: it should be
drawn as world geometry, in R8's records, through R7b's culling, with a pose.

**What this builds on:** M50d (`Body_Shape`, `Body_Motion`, the client's body list and its
interpolated pose, the body mesh per revision, the `body` visual baseline, the `bodies`
scenario: 16 bodies of 4 096 blocks moving in view, its `frame_record` limit and `gpu_held`
reported); R7b (`world_cull.comp`, the two phases, `--check occlusion`), R8 (the quad record,
`quad.glsl`), R9b and R10 (the arena, the ring, `in_flight`), R11 (the graph), R11c (the sway
margin), R15 (instances).

**Checked against the code and M50d's spec (2026-10-30, #87):**

- Until M50d is built, the names below are its spec's; the session checks them against the
  code M50d left and corrects this spec in its first commit (the plan's rule for specs written
  ahead).
- **A body's light**: M50d lights a body "with the light at its pivot's cell (no light through
  or from its blocks while moving)", so its quads' baked light is not the light it is drawn
  with: the pose record carries the body's light, and the body pipelines use it instead of the
  record's per-corner light (AO from the record stays).
- **The world pass's records have no pose.** `Chunk_Draw` (R8: the chunk, 12 bytes) places a
  mesh by its chunk; a body piece needs a position (a chunk and a fraction, as the camera-
  relative scheme requires) and a yaw, changing every frame. So bodies get a table of their own
  (below) rather than widening every chunk's record.
- **Culling a rotated piece**: the chunk box test of R6 and R7 assumes an axis-aligned 16-block
  box at a chunk position. A body piece's bounds are the box around its rotated cells; a
  property test proves the bound contains every vertex.
- **Water in bodies**: water is drawn from the CPU's sorted list. A body's translucent layer
  (if M50c lets a body carry water at all; checked at step 0) stays on the held path, drawn
  after the world's water as now; R15b moves opaque and cutout only.

## Design (R15b)

### Body pieces as world meshes

- A body piece's quads are R8 records in the world's arena (uploaded through R10's ring, once
  per revision), with a `Body_Piece_Draw` record (`client/render/world_pass.odin`): the
  piece's offset inside the body (cells), its body's index in the pose table, and its first
  quad. A separate dense table `Body_Cull_Record` (the piece's local bounds, its first quad,
  its face-group counts), at most `MAX_BODIES` (64) x 8 pieces.
- **The pose table** (per frame slot, host-visible, written by the CPU each frame from M50d's
  interpolated pose): per body, its position as the camera chunk's offset (`f32`, computed from
  `f64` on the CPU) and its yaw's sine and cosine, its light (sky, block), 32 bytes.

### Pipelines and culling

- `world_body.vert` (sharing `quad.glsl` with `world.vert`): the record's corner in the
  piece, plus the piece's offset, rotated by the body's yaw about its pivot, plus the body's
  position; the body's light replaces the corners' light. Opaque and cutout pipelines (`world
  body`, `world body cutout`), drawn in the same graph passes as the world's layers, after
  them.
- `body_cull.comp` (or a mode of `world_cull.comp`, decided by which keeps `--check cull`
  simpler): per piece, its rotated bounds (the eight corners of its local box turned and moved:
  an axis-aligned box around them) against the frustum and, in R7b's phase 2, the pyramid; the
  same two phases and visibility bits (slots of their own after the chunks'). **Face groups are
  not used for bodies** (a rotated piece's faces do not face the axes): one command per layer
  per piece, all faces, back-face culling doing the rest.
- Bodies write depth like the world: they occlude and are occluded. `--check occlusion`'s
  route gains moving bodies (below).

### Leaving the held path

M50d's body drawing through `held_world_begin` and `Held_Push`'s model matrix is removed for
opaque and cutout; the CPU's per-body frustum test is removed. The `body` baseline must pass
unchanged.

## The order of the steps (R15b)

0. Check M50d as built (names, the body mesh, the pose, water in bodies); correct this spec.
1. The pose table, `Body_Piece_Draw`, uploads; `world_body.vert` and its pipelines; drawn
   without culling; `images`' `body` scene unchanged.
2. `body_cull.comp`, both phases; `--check cull` extended to pieces.
3. The held path removed for bodies; `--check occlusion` with bodies; measurement; docs;
   history.md (R15 and R15b's specs appended).

## Tests (R15b)

- `client/render/render_test.odin`:
  - `test_body_bounds_contain_every_vertex` (property, fixed seed 87, 2 000 cases): random
    piece contents, offsets, yaws and positions; every expanded vertex (CPU, `quad_corner` then
    the body transform) lies inside the bounds the cull uses.
  - `test_body_pose_record`: position and yaw from an `f64` pose 30 000 blocks from the origin
    give the same vertex as M50d's held-path matrix within 1e-4 block.
- `--check cull` (extended): pieces' commands, bits and counts against `cull_reference`'s body
  rules for 64 bodies at random poses, both phases, with a pyramid from `hiz_reference`.
- `--check bodies` (`client/render/bodies_check.odin`, CI, lavapipe, validation): a compute
  shader including `world_body`'s transform expands every piece of a lift and a door at eight
  yaws; every vertex equal (within 1e-4 block) to the held path's reference transform (kept as
  `body_vertices_reference`).
- `--check occlusion` (extended route): two lifts moving between floors and a door swinging in
  view: no false occlusion, of pieces or of chunks behind them.
- `images`: `body` unchanged (pictures and counts except `world_meshes`, which now counts
  pieces: re-accepted count, said).

## Benchmarks (R15b)

`bench compare <the commit before R15b> bodies` (M50d's scenario), `views`:

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| `bodies` `frame_record` | lower (p < 0.05): the bodies' CPU work is a pose table |
| `bodies` `gpu_world` + `gpu_held` | not worse (the bodies moved from one to the other) |
| `bodies` `chunks_occluded` | reported (bodies now hide what is behind them) |
| `views` | within noise (no bodies) |

M50d's limit (the bodies' share of `frame_record` under 0.2 ms) is re-measured and reported.

## Left out (R15b)

Water carried by bodies on the world pass; bodies at a level of detail; light through or from
a moving body (M50c's rule); pitch and roll (M50c's bodies only yaw).

## Exit check (R15b)

**Gated in CI:** `--check bodies`, `--check cull` with pieces and `--check occlusion` with
moving bodies pass on lavapipe under validation; the bounds property test; `images`' `body`
scene unchanged; `build check` and the suite. **Measured** (development machine, reported):
`bodies` `frame_record` lower with p < 0.05, the GPU cost not worse.
