# R7: the depth pyramid and the occlusion test (built, checked against the CPU; nothing culled yet)

The first of two occlusion rows (R7, [R7b](R7b.md); split by #87: the pyramid, the test and
the frame's rendering split are one session, the two-phase cull in the frame and its checks
another). Arc rules: [R.md](R.md#rules-for-every-r-milestone), and what the renderer is now:
[R.md, after R5b](R.md#where-the-renderer-stands-after-r5b-checked-against-the-code-2026-10-30-87).

**Why.** 72% of the chunks drawn at the spawn cover no pixel (M19's occlusion statistics). R7b
culls them with a hierarchical depth buffer; before that, the depth buffer has to be readable,
the pyramid built, and the test proved conservative (it never hides what can be seen),
because an occlusion bug shows as a hole in the world that only some cameras see.

**Checked against the code (2026-10-30, #87; corrections to the old R7 text):**

- **The reduction is a minimum, not a maximum.** The old text said "max-reduction for
  reverse Z: the pyramid holds the farthest depth". In reverse Z (near 1, far 0) the
  farthest depth of a footprint is its **minimum**; a maximum pyramid would hide visible
  chunks. Fixed here; `--check hiz` and the property test below catch it.
- **The depth image cannot be sampled and is not kept.** `Swapchain.depth` and
  `Offscreen.depth` have usage `DEPTH_STENCIL_ATTACHMENT` only and `begin_rendering` stores
  depth with `DONT_CARE`. They gain `SAMPLED` and `STORE`.
- **There is one depth image, not one per frame slot**, shared by both frames in flight
  (their work is ordered on the one queue, with the barrier in `begin_frame`). So is the
  pyramid: it is built and read inside one frame's command buffer. The old "per frame slot"
  is dropped.
- **There are no storage-image descriptors.** The bindless set holds sampled images and
  samplers, and every pipeline shares one layout (`g.pipeline_layout`). The pyramid's compute
  pass gets a pipeline layout of its own (the bindless set, then a set per mip level), so no
  new device feature is needed (rule 5).
- **The world is drawn inside one rendering scope** with everything else. The pyramid must
  be built from the world's opaque and cutout depth alone (the view model clears depth, the
  sky and water are drawn after), so the frame's rendering is split after `world_draw`.
- The old step "(4) `debug.occlusion_stats` reports culled counts from the new path" and the
  metric `chunks_occluded` are R7b's (they need the culling).

## Design (R7)

### Scene counts (rule 8; the arc's CI view of a frame's work)

`client/scenes.odin`: when a scene takes its picture it writes `images/<scene>.counts` from
the frame the picture is of (the cull pass's statistics as read back, `World_Stats`,
`Draw_Counts`): `world_meshes`, `world_triangles`, `draw_calls`, `api_draw_calls`,
`world_api_draw_calls`, `frame_triangles`. `tools/build/images.odin`: `images_counts_parse`,
`images_counts_compare` (equal, or within a `~tolerance`), the comparison after the picture's,
a failure naming the count, the baseline and the value; `--accept` writes both files. All
eleven scenes' counts are accepted in this row (the pictures do not change).

### The frame's rendering split

`gpu.begin_rendering(frame, clear_color, load := false)`: with `load`, colour and depth are
loaded, not cleared. `begin_frame` keeps clearing; depth is stored (`STORE`) in every scope.
`client/app.odin`'s frame becomes: rendering A (clear; `world_draw`) → `end_rendering` → the
pyramid → rendering B (`load`; entities, held, sky, water, debug, view model, UI, as now).
Two layout transitions per frame: depth `DEPTH_ATTACHMENT_OPTIMAL` → `SHADER_READ_ONLY_OPTIMAL`
(after late fragment tests) and back (before early fragment tests), in `client/render/hiz.odin`
with synchronization2 barriers naming the exact stages and accesses.

### The pyramid (`client/render/hiz.odin`, `assets/shaders/hiz.comp`)

- `Hiz :: struct {image: gpu.Image, mips: u32, size: [2]u32, views: [HIZ_MAX_MIPS]vk.ImageView,
  sets: [HIZ_MAX_MIPS]vk.DescriptorSet, layout, pool, pipeline}`; `hiz_init`, `hiz_resize(g,
  depth_size)` (called where the swapchain and the offscreen images are remade: after the GPU
  is idle, so its descriptor sets are written there and never while a frame uses them),
  `hiz_build(h, g, cmd, depth)`, `hiz_destroy`.
- Image `R32_SFLOAT` (storage-image support for it is mandatory in Vulkan 1.0), usage
  `STORAGE | SAMPLED`, layout `GENERAL` for its whole life.
- **Sizes.** Mip 0 is the largest power of two at or below the depth image's size, on each
  axis separately (1280 x 720 gives 1024 x 512); each mip below halves each axis down to 1.
  A mip-0 texel `(x, y)` covers depth texels `[floor(x * W / w0), ceil((x + 1) * W / w0))`
  across and likewise down, so every depth texel is in some footprint (conservative even when
  the size is not a power of two). Mips 1 and below reduce exactly 2 x 2.
- **The reduction is the minimum** (the farthest depth in reverse Z). One dispatch per mip,
  8 x 8 invocations a group; a compute-to-compute barrier (storage write → storage read)
  between mips. Mip 0 reads the depth image (`texelFetch` on its sampled view, set 1
  binding 0 for mip 0); mip `i` reads mip `i - 1` (`imageLoad`) and writes mip `i`
  (`imageStore`): set `i` holds those two views. No subgroup operations, no sampler
  reduction (rule 5).
- Label `depth pyramid`, metric `gpu_hiz` (`base.Metric.Gpu_Hiz`, `GPU_SECTION_METRICS`).
- Device: `DEPTH_FORMAT` must have `SAMPLED_IMAGE` in `optimalTilingFeatures`; checked in
  `supports_required_features`, refused with a log line naming it (rule 5).

### The occlusion test (`assets/shaders/occlusion.glsl`, its twin `occlusion_reference`)

One function, included by the check's shader here and by `world_cull.comp` in R7b:
`bool occluded(mat4 view_proj, vec3 lo, vec3 hi, Hiz_Info hiz)` with `Hiz_Info` the mip-0
size, the mip count and the pyramid's sampled view id. The rules (and the CPU's, in
`hiz.odin`, written from the same text):

1. Project the box's eight corners. If any has `w <= HIZ_NEAR_W` (a corner at or behind
   the near plane): not occluded.
2. The corners' NDC rectangle, clamped to the screen, scaled to mip-0 texels; its extent
   `e` in texels on each axis. If the rectangle is empty after clamping: not occluded (the
   frustum test owns that case).
3. The level `l = clamp(ceil(log2(max(e.x, e.y))), 0, mips - 1)`: at level `l` the rectangle
   covers at most 2 x 2 texels. Fetch those (clamped to the mip's size); the farthest depth
   there is their minimum.
4. The box's nearest depth is the maximum of its corners' `z / w`. **Occluded when the box's
   nearest depth is below the pyramid's farthest** (strictly).

### `--check hiz` (`client/render/hiz_check.odin`, `cull_check.odin`'s pattern; rule 9)

On lavapipe in CI, outside the frames (`gpu.one_shot_begin`): (a) **the pyramid**: for depth
sizes 640 x 360, 1280 x 720, 1366 x 768 (uneven footprints), 3 x 2 and 1 x 1, depth images
filled by a buffer-to-image copy from a fixed hash (values in [0, 1]) and from a CPU raster of
boxes, the GPU's pyramid read back and compared with `hiz_reference` (the same footprints and
minimum on the CPU), every texel of every mip **bit-exact** (a minimum of floats is exact);
(b) **the test**: 4 096 generated boxes, four cameras, against a pyramid of the raster; every
GPU verdict equal to `occlusion_reference`'s, the generator rejecting boxes within `1e-5` of
the depth comparison or `1e-3` texel of a footprint edge, and failing as "not a test" unless at
least a quarter of the verdicts are each way. A row in `exit_check.odin`'s `CHECKS`; a CI step
"Depth pyramid check" beside "Culling check".

## The order of the steps (R7)

1. Scene counts: the writer, the comparison, the accepted `.counts` for the eleven scenes,
   `.gitattributes`; `images_test.odin` cases (equal passes, a changed exact count fails, a
   toleranced count within and outside its tolerance).

3. `Hiz`, `hiz.comp`, its pipeline layout and sets, `hiz_resize` at the swapchain and offscreen

   measured here, not mixed into R7b's).
4. `occlusion.glsl`, `occlusion_reference`, `hiz_reference`; `--check hiz`; the CI step.
5. The property test; the metric; docs/debugging.md (Graphics: the pyramid, the split; the
   check); the cost table; history.md.

## Tests (R7)

- `client/render/hiz_test.odin`:
  - `test_hiz_sizes`: mip-0 size and mip count for 1 x 1, 3 x 2, 640 x 360, 1280 x 720,
    1366 x 768, 2560 x 1440, 7680 x 4320; the footprints of mip 0 cover every depth texel
    exactly once or more, and none outside the image.
  - `test_hiz_reference_is_the_minimum`: a pyramid of a known 8 x 4 depth image, by hand.
  - `test_occlusion_reference_cases`: a box behind a nearer wall is occluded; the same box
    beside the wall is not; a box crossing the near plane is not; a box larger than the
    screen is tested at the coarsest level; a box exactly at the wall's depth is not.
  - `test_occlusion_is_conservative` (a property test, fixed seed 87, 500 scenes): a CPU
    raster of 1 to 40 random occluder boxes into a depth buffer of a random size (16 x 9 to
    320 x 180) with a random camera, its pyramid by `hiz_reference`, 200 random test boxes;
    every test box with at least one pixel nearer than the buffer there (the raster's own
    rule) must be "not occluded". It is the oracle for the whole design: a maximum reduction,
    a level one too fine, or a footprint that misses a texel each fail it (tried by hand, said
    in the report). Not a Q1 fuzz target: it parses no input.
- `tools/build/images_test.odin`: the counts cases of step 1.
- `--check hiz` (CI), `--check cull` (unchanged), `images` with counts (CI).

## Benchmarks (R7)

`bench compare <the commit before R7> views` and `views -size:2560x1440`: `gpu_hiz` reported
for both sizes; `gpu_frame` may rise by about `gpu_hiz` (the split adds a store of depth and a
reload: reported). Budget, decided here as the professional bar for a pyramid this size:
`gpu_hiz` at most 0.10 ms at 1280 x 720 and 0.30 ms at 2560 x 1440 on the development machine;
over it, the build is made cheaper (fewer dispatches: two mips per dispatch through shared
memory), never the budget raised. `flight` frame p99 within noise.

## Validation and synchronization

`VX_VALIDATION=1` (with synchronization validation) clean on `--check hiz`, `--check blast`
and a `views --smoke` run; the depth transitions and the per-mip barriers are what
synchronization validation checks; a deliberately removed barrier is tried once and must be
reported by the layer (said in the report).

## Left out (R7)

Culling with the pyramid (R7b); occlusion of water; a single-pass pyramid with subgroup
operations or atomics (only if the budget is missed); reading the pyramid for anything else
(screen-space effects are R11b's, from the depth image itself).

## Exit check (R7)

**Gated in CI:** `--check hiz` passes on lavapipe under validation; `test_occlusion_is_conservative`
and the other `hiz_test.odin` tests pass; `images` passes with every scene's counts compared
(pictures unchanged, counts accepted in this row); `--check cull`, `--check blast`, the smoke
benchmarks, `build check` and the suite. **Measured** (development machine, reported):
`bench compare <the commit before R7> views` and `-size:2560x1440` with `gpu_hiz` within the
budget above.
