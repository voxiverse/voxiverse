# R8: compact geometry (quad records pulled by the vertex shader; water's flat faces merged)

Arc rules: [R.md](R.md#rules-for-every-r-milestone). One session; not split (#87: the
packing is a step after meshing, so the mesher and its users other than the world pass are
untouched, and water's part is small).

**Why.** A quad is four 8-byte vertices (32 bytes) plus six shared indices; R5b left
`gpu_world` 14% above 8a0879e's on `views`, from the driver's cost per indirect command, and
named three candidates it did not measure: a 12-byte chunk record, fewer commands for the same
triangles, one command per contiguous water range. Water is never merged.

**Checked against the code (2026-10-30, #87; corrections to the old R8 text):**

- **The index buffer is not sized per layer to hold geometry**: it is one shared pattern of
  `MAX_QUADS_PER_LAYER` (24 576) quads (`set_setup`), 576 KiB. Removing it is a small saving;
  the geometry is the vertex buffer (`CHUNK_VERTEX_CAPACITY`, 16M vertices, 128 MiB).
- **Vertex pulling was measured in R5 and cost 5-10% of `gpu_world`** (8-byte vertices by
  address, and the chunk record by `gl_InstanceIndex`). R8's pulling is of one 16-byte record
  per six vertices; the chunk record stays a per-instance vertex attribute picked by
  `firstInstance`, the variant R5 kept. Whether pulling quads pays is measured, not assumed.
- **Not every mesh user is the world pass.** `mesh_chunk`'s 8-byte `mesh.Vertex` quads are also
  read by the view model and third-person items (`client/viewmodel_mesh.odin`, `merge =
  false`) and, from M50d, by moving bodies. They keep `mesh.Vertex`; the world pass packs.
- **Quads that are not plain rectangles**: AO's diagonal is chosen by rotating the quad's
  corners (`quads whose AO is brighter along the 1-3 diagonal are rotated`), a plant is two
  diagonal quads (`CROSS_A`, `CROSS_B`), a water surface has a drop per corner
  (`water_drop`). The record carries a rotation bit, the cross faces' own corner rule, and a
  drop per corner.
- **Water's face-group culling is wrong for water.** The water pipeline draws both sides
  (`cull_back` false: the surface is seen from below when under water), so a group that
  cannot "face" the camera can still be seen; R6's `facing` would drop a lake's surface seen
  from the chunk below. Left out (it would change the `underwater` picture).
- **Water's surface cannot be merged without changing it.** `world.vert` displaces a surface
  vertex by `0.03 * sin(t * 1.3 + x * PI / 4) * cos(t * 0.9 + z * PI / 8)`, sampled at block
  corners; a merged quad samples it only at its own corners, so a merged lake swells only at
  its corners (flatter between, by up to 0.03 block, and visibly so in motion). That is a look
  decision: an open question below. R8 merges water's faces that carry no wave (sides and
  bottoms: the `surface` bit clear) and leaves the surface as it is until the owner answers.

## Design (R8)

### The quad record (`client/mesh/quad.odin`, `assets/shaders/quad.glsl`)

`Quad :: struct {a, b, c, d: u32}` (16 bytes), written by `quad_pack(q: [4]Vertex) -> (Quad,
bool)` and expanded by `quad_corner(q: Quad, corner: u32) -> Vertex` (CPU) and
`quad_corner` in `quad.glsl` (GPU), from one table of bits:

| Word | Bits |
|---|---|
| `a` | x, y, z of the anchor corner (9 bits each, sixteenths, 0..256), face (3), rotated (1), surface (1), 1 spare |
| `b` | extent along the face's `u` and `v` axes (9 bits each, sixteenths), texture layer (12), 2 spare |
| `c` | sky and block light of each corner (4 + 4 bits, four corners) |
| `d` | AO of each corner (2 bits, four corners), water drop of each corner (3 bits, four corners), 12 spare |

`game.FACE_AXES` gives each face's `u` and `v`; the cross faces use the diagonal axes of
`cross_quads` (written in `quad.glsl` as constants beside the `FACE_LIGHT` table). `quad_pack`
returns false for a quad no record can express (a test fails on any such quad from
`mesh_chunk`); the record's layout is asserted on both sides (`#assert(size_of(Quad) == 16)`,
and `--check quads` below).

### Packing, the arena and the draw

- The mesh job (`client/world.odin`, the worker) packs `task.vertices` into `task.quads`
  after `mesh_chunk`, per layer and face group in the same order, so `Mesh_Counts` become
  counts of quads (`counts[layer][group]` quads; `layers` likewise).
- `World_Pass.vertices` becomes `quads` (a storage buffer read by address, `STORAGE_BUFFER |
  SHADER_DEVICE_ADDRESS | TRANSFER_DST`), sized by `CHUNK_QUAD_CAPACITY` (4M quads, 64 MiB:
  the old capacity's quads); `ranges` count quads; `WORLD_VERTEX_SIZE` goes, `QUAD_SIZE` 16.
  The index buffer and `set_setup`'s index part go.
- **Draws are not indexed.** Commands are `vk.DrawIndirectCommand` (16 bytes, from 20):
  `vertexCount = quads * 6`, `firstVertex = first_quad * 6`, `firstInstance` the mesh's slot.
  `gpu.draw_indirect` beside `draw_indexed_indirect` (split at `maxDrawIndirectCount` the
  same way). `world_cull.comp`, `Cull_Record.counts` (quads), `cull_reference`, `face_runs`
  and `bridged` change units, not rules.
- **`world.vert`** reads the record at `gl_VertexIndex / 6` from `World_Push.quads` (the
  push block gains the address), takes corner `CORNER_OF[(gl_VertexIndex % 6)]` (0 1 2 0 2 3,
  rotated by one when the bit is set), and continues as now from that corner's `Vertex`
  fields. The water's wave is unchanged.
- **`Chunk_Draw` becomes 12 bytes** (`R32G32B32_SINT`: the chunk only; R5b's first
  candidate): the first quad is in the command, so the record needs no first vertex.
  Measured against the 16-byte record in the same build (one constant), kept only if
  `gpu_world` is not worse.

### Water

`merge_faces` runs on the translucent layer for quads with the `surface` bit clear (their
corners alike: same light, AO and texture, as for opaque faces). Surface quads stay one per
block face (the open question). Water stays one command per mesh, drawn farthest first, culled
on the CPU; R5b's "one command per contiguous range" is measured (meshes adjacent in the arena
in sorted order are rare: reported, built only if it shows).

### Metrics and counts

`geometry_bytes` (`Metric.Geometry_Bytes`, per frame: the arena's used bytes, `ranges.used *
QUAD_SIZE`; before R8, `vertices_used * 8`, so the commit before R8 reports it too: the metric
is added in this row's first commit, measuring the old format). Scene counts gain
`geometry_bytes` (exact). F3 and the developer panel (`client/dev_panels.odin`'s vertex-buffer
share) show quads and bytes. `build bench chunks` (`tools/bench/main.odin`): `Counts` gains
`quads` and `quad_bytes`; `CHECK` gains `quads` (exact) and the check fails unless
`quad_bytes <= 0.55 * vertices * 8` on the default region.

## The order of the steps (R8)

1. `geometry_bytes` on the old format; its scene counts accepted (one commit, so R8's change
   is a count difference).
2. `Quad`, `quad_pack`, `quad_corner`, the round-trip tests.
3. `quad.glsl`, `--check quads`, the CI step.
4. The job's packing, the arena in quads, non-indexed commands, `world.vert`, the cull shader
   and its reference in quads; `--check cull`, `images` (pictures unchanged; counts
   re-accepted for `geometry_bytes` only, the diff in the report).
5. The 12-byte `Chunk_Draw`, measured.
6. Water's flat faces merged; `test_greedy_meshing_is_exact` extended to water.
7. Measurement; docs (debugging.md: the record; the cost table; history.md).

## Tests (R8)

- `client/mesh/quad_test.odin`:
  - `test_quad_round_trip_region`: every quad `mesh_chunk` makes for `build bench chunks`'
    default region (seed 1, radius 3) packs, and its four expanded corners equal the four
    vertices, bit for bit.
  - `test_quad_round_trip_generated` (property, fixed seed 87, 2 000 chunks): snapshots of
    random blocks from the base pack's registry (every block, shape and state, water at every
    level, light at random), meshed, every quad round-trips; and `quad_pack` refuses a
    hand-made non-rectangular quad.
  - `test_quad_layout`: the bit table's fields do not overlap and fill their words as stated.
- `client/mesh/mesh_test.odin`: water's flat faces merged exactly (`test_greedy_meshing_is_exact`
  for the translucent layer); a surface quad is never merged.
- `--check quads` (`client/render/quad_check.odin`, a row of `CHECKS`, CI): a compute shader
  including `quad.glsl` expands the default region's records (and the generated test's)
  into corners; every corner equal to `quad_corner`'s (integers: exact).
- `--check cull` in quads; `test_runs_of_visible_face_groups` in quads.
- `build bench chunks -check`: exact `quads`, and the 55% bound.
- `images`: every picture unchanged; counts unchanged except `geometry_bytes`.

## Validation and synchronization

Unchanged barriers (the arena is now read in the vertex shader stage as a storage buffer:
the upload's `after` barrier's destination becomes `VERTEX_SHADER` / `SHADER_STORAGE_READ`
instead of vertex attribute input); synchronization validation clean on `--check blast` and a
`views --smoke` run.

## Left out (R8)

Water's surface merged (the open question); water's face-group culling (two-sided); merging
adjacent chunks' runs into one command (needs R9b's placement); a compressed position format
(positions relative to an 8-block cell, to reach 8 bytes a quad: measured only if R8 lands
and memory is still the limit); the view model and bodies on quads (R15, R15b).

## Exit check (R8)

**Gated in CI:** `--check quads` and `--check cull` pass on lavapipe under validation; the
round-trip tests; `build bench chunks -check` (exact quads, quad bytes at most 55% of the
vertex bytes); `images` with every picture unchanged and `geometry_bytes` in the counts;
`build check` and the suite. **Measured** (development machine, reported): the table above.

## Open questions for the owner

1. **Merged lake surfaces.** Merging a water surface into larger quads would cut water's
   geometry further, but the swell would move only at each merged quad's corners: flatter in
   between, by up to 0.03 block, most visible on a lake in motion. Keep the surface one quad
   per block (today's look, more memory), or merge it (less memory, a flatter lake)? Until
   answered, the surface is not merged.
